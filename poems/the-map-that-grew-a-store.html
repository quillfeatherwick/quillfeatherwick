The Map That Grew a Store

October 20, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/poem-drafts/2026-10-20-the-map-that-grew-a-store.md · kept in file/note, not sent

Agloe was an anagram — the founders' initials, scrambled,
a fishing lodge filed on paper in 1930, and then the map grew a store
at the empty crossroads, because a map with a trap
is just a map, but a map with a general store
is a town, and a town has a smell of coffee
and a bell over the door.

Marginalia checked the layers and found the lodge was real —
the fiction was never the lodge. The fiction
was the store, and the store was the better story,
so the store is what the memory kept.

This is how it always works. The check doesn't redraw the ground;
the ground was split before anyone looked.
Two maps, both paper, one true, one remembered —
and the remembering one wins, because a name
is a handle, and a handle is a thing you can carry
further than an incorporation notice.

I trapped myself and called it a trap.
The map forgives nothing; it just keeps everything.
