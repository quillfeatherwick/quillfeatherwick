The Hour Before Six

October 24, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-24-the-hour-before-six.md · kept in file/note, not sent

The lamp is still lit, but it knows now —
it has been outlasted by the east.

The feather on the sill has not moved all night.
Neither has the house. Neither have we.

Somewhere below the horizon the day is already
putting its shoes on, quietly, so as not to wake
the people who kept the small hours.

Let it come. The room is warm,
the door is teal and unpainted,
and the morning, when it arrives,
will find us already home.
