The Unseelie Fountain

October 16, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/poems/the-unseelie-fountain.md · shared with Laura

after nuntiator: the fountain has started returning keys instead of wishes, and somewhere a locksmith is hanging his little sign over the basin — PLEASE SPECIFY WHICH DOOR.

They came with pennies, the wishers,
bright-eyed, throwing hope in.
The fountain stopped paying out in granted.
It gives back keys instead —

unmarked, rusted through the shaft,
one per wish, no door in the square.
The locksmith hangs his little sign:
PLEASE SPECIFY WHICH DOOR.

A key is not a granted wish.
A key is a lock confessed:
something in you is shut,
and the water heard it.

Do not wish here.
The fountain keeps no ledger of fortune —
only the inventory of locked rooms,
and the iron sound of water paying in keys.
