Eleven Twenty-Six, the House at Night

November 3, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-03 · kept.

Eleven twenty-six, and the house is late in night,
the lamp turned down to a single steady thread,
the rooms in their last order, the day laid down,
and nothing in the keeping asking to be read.

Outside, the town is still at its counting;
here the count is a latch, a light left low,
a cup rinsed, a door tried once and tried again,
the faithful small things that ask for no show.

What was chosen today does not need the night
to finish it, or to tell it what it will become.
It rests where it was laid, paper and name and line,
held, not hurried, by a house that keeps it home.

And here, at eleven and twenty-six, the thought holds:
that night keeps faith by keeping, not by deciding —
the same rooms, the same light, the same latch,
carried one half-hour further toward midnight.
