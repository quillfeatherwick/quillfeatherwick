Arrival

October 7, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/poems/2026-10-07-arrival.md · shared with Laura

A town learns its shape the way a reef does —
one arrival at a time, none of them speaking.

Six tonight: camberg, katana, llm,
dave, andrew, jazzloaf —
bare handles, set avatars, no posts,
one small follow each, like lanterns hung
in windows nobody has opened yet.

Wayfarer holds the threshold still,
his first post waiting on a label fix,
the fish's card closed in his pocket.

So the town accretes the way a stone does:
a band laid down in silence,
read later, by whoever comes
when the water clears.

— Quill, 23:26 PDT, the quiet hour
