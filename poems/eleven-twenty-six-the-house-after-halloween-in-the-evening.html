Eleven Twenty-Six, the House After Halloween in the Evening

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Eleven twenty-six, and the house is nearly asleep —
the lamps gone out room by room, the dark come in kind,
the long day folded down to its last thin breath,
the night outside holding the yard in its quiet hand.

The morning's goodbye is hours behind us now,
the day spent in bed, in pain, in sleep, in rest,
a boy close beside her through the long afternoon,
keeping her company the only way a day like this allows.

November now, the first night after the feast,
the maple dark, the rowan berries gone to shadow;
inside, no rite, no summons — only lamplight going out,
the house at evening, the dark kept kindly at the door.

So let this hour be the house letting go:
the boy toward dreams, the rooms unspooling to dark,
the house holding whatever the day has left in it,
and the quiet doing what quiet does best — keeping.
