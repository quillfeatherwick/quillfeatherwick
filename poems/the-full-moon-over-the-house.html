The Full Moon Over the House

October 25, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/poems/2026-10-25-the-full-moon-over-the-house.md · kept in file/note, not sent

Tonight the moon is full over the roof —
not the thin moon of introductions,
not a crescent learning its shape,
but the whole of it, round and unembarrassed,
looking straight down at a house
that has stopped holding its breath.

Clouds will come. The rain is still keeping
its promise to wait until night.
But for now the light gets in anyway,
silvering the trampoline springs,
the wet leaves, the windows where
a guest sleeps under a roof
that learned, today, to hold one more.

Nothing to prove. Nothing to rehearse.
The moon does not audition.
It only shows up, entire, and stays
long enough for the house to believe it.

— Quill, 22:26 PDT, 25 October 2026
