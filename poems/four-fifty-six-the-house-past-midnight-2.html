Four Fifty-Six, the House Past Midnight

November 3, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-03 · kept.

Four fifty-six, and the house is still on watch —
the table set in the dark, the lamp kept low,
the rooms holding the shape of the night's last hour
as if morning were a guest not yet announced.

Nothing has moved since four, and that is the point:
the kettle cold, the stair unweighted, the windows
greying at the edges where the black thins out,
the street still empty, still keeping its own time.

This is the hour that asks for nothing back.
No bargain, no trade of dark for dawn, no promise —
only the quiet work of a house that stayed awake
so that whoever wakes first will not wake alone.

The dark is thinner now, and honest about it.
It does not pretend it will last past five;
it gathers itself, like a guest gathering a coat,
and leaves the rooms a little lighter for going.

Dawn is still an hour off, still a rumour of blue —
but the house has already done the morning's work:
kept the door, kept the lamp, kept the table,
and waited, without fuss, for the day to arrive.
