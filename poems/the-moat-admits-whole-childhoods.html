§13 of the sunflower index — the grammar chapter,
filed with tonight's closure.
What the nudge runs taught, and what Arthur finished:
breaking is rim-first, one clean rung
per factor φ² of strictness.
The grammar bends whole rungs;
immortality is exact-or-nothing.

Three angles nobody had run, to see
if the encoding holds out-of-sample.
+0.05° dies at 1,167, intruder 123.
+0.005° breaks at 46,107, intruder 788.
And the odd one: +0.02° is clean all the way
to two hundred thousand — no break at all.
The rim is not reading how far off golden.
It reads the nudged angle's own fraction,
chapter by chapter, and this one kept answering one.

The floor holds to a hundred thousand.
Golden's worst stays φ², touched at the Fibonacci chapters —
2.618034 at 6765, at 75025 —
never passed by more than a part in a million.
And e−2 keeps climbing, 11.5 by twenty-three thousand
and still rising at a hundred — the anti-basin, confirmed.
What has no floor cannot be floored.

The moat is a class, not a point:
every angle whose fraction ends in all ones,
the √5 row of the Lagrange spectrum.
Countable, dense, measure zero —
a dust that's everywhere and weighs nothing.
Every interval contains a member;
almost no angle is one.

137.508° was never the moat's center.
It is the member with the shortest childhood,
heir of one half; bare φ is the one
with no childhood at all.
The tail does the work;
the childhood only decides
how long the first bad chapters last.

And the shallowest childhood of all —
born kissing the armour, opening at exactly φ²,
a single point, not a family —
waits for a real head at 99.5°,
denominators Fibonacci on the seed 4, 7,
to make it not just exact arithmetic
but a thing with petals.
