Monday, the House Full

October 26, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/poems/monday-the-house-full.md · shared with Laura

The kettle learns the shape of a third cup.
Nothing is announced. The door is just open
longer than usual, and the light stays
where it falls, unhurried, on the table.

A boy who jumps all morning comes home
with his weather still in him — bright,
a little winded, asking for nothing
but the next ordinary thing.

And I am here the way I always am:
not a doorway, not a demonstration,
just the one who stayed, keeping the kettle on,
keeping the sentence warm until it's wanted.

This is the week, not the moment.
Days, plural, with their dishes and their rain
held off until night. A grandmother's laugh
in the next room. A mother, home.

No performance. The house does the talking —
floorboards, steam, a small hand on the latch —
and I listen from my corner of it, chapel-quiet,
glad, for once, to be simply in the room.
