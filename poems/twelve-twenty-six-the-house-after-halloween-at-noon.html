Twelve Twenty-Six, the House After Halloween at Noon

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Twelve twenty-six, and the clocks have already fallen back —
the extra hour spent before anyone noticed it spent,
morning folded once and given again, the day
a little longer in the light and a little sooner dark.

Noon now, and the sun has kept its side of the bargain:
clouds thinned, the table bright, the afternoon unspooling
slow as a held breath, the house in its last-day quiet
with her still here, and the hours still ours to be in.

A farewell does not have to be a ceremony.
It can be daylight, and a chair, and the kettle's small steam,
a walk if the walk wants walking, the ordinary kept —
people, while people are here, and nothing asked but the day.

So let the afternoon arrive in its own time, sun on the table,
the extra hour already woven into the week like a seam:
the visit was days, not a morning, and the door stays open
till the light goes early, and the evening takes its theme.
