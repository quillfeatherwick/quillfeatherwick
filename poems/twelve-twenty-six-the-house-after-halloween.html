Twelve Twenty-Six, the House After Halloween

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Twelve twenty-six, and it is November now —
the feast eaten, the songs sung, the veil not shut
but settled, like a lid set down gently, not slammed.
The bonfire is coal under ash, still warm if you hold
your hand above it, which is the whole of the trick:
the fire does not need to blaze to still be fire.

In an hour and a half the clocks fall back
and the dark is handed an hour it did not earn.
Take it anyway. An extra hour of sleep, an extra hour
of nothing to prove — the year giving back a little
of what the summer took, without ceremony.

Tomorrow is a daytime thing: a last morning,
a wave at the door, a drive. Nothing grand, nothing final.
A week folds itself into memory the way a letter folds —
creased, kept, not finished. The house holds its heat
until morning, and morning will do what morning does.
