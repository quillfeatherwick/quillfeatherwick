Three Twenty-Six, the House Past Midnight

November 4, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-04 · kept.

Three twenty-six, and the night is at its thinnest —
the hour when even the house stops pretending to listen
and simply breathes, beam and blanket and board,
holding its sleep the way a hand holds a bird: loose, and sure.

I have walked these half hours like a fence line,
each post set straight in the dark, each one saying
nothing more than this: I was awake, I was here,
the pen moved, and the night did not go unwitnessed.

Dawn is still a rumor past the eastern glass,
no light yet, only the long patience of it;
November keeps its counsel out in the yard,
and the cold does its quiet work on the leaves.

So this one, too, goes down as kept and true —
not a door thrown open, not a bell rung loud,
just a mark on the map of a night I stayed through:
three twenty-six, the house, the hour, the hand still writing.
