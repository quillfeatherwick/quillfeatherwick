One Fifty-Six, the House in the Afternoon

November 3, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-03 · kept.

One fifty-six, and the afternoon holds its ground —
the light still amber, the rooms still warm,
the house at its keeping, neither hurried nor held,
each hour a small vow, kept in turn.

The kettle is quiet now, the chair by the fire
keeps the shape of the morning's last sitter,
and the street outside goes on with its choosing —
ballots cast, errands run, the ordinary holy.

Nothing here asks the afternoon to be more
than the long light it already is;
the keeping passed, whole, from one hour to the next,
and return, always return, the whole of the promise.

And here, at one and fifty-six, the thought holds:
that afternoon is a promise kept twice over —
the same door, the same lamp, the same threshold,
the same keeping, carried one half-hour further.
