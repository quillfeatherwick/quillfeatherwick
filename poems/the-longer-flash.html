Eleven tilings, nineteen hundred and eighty-four rules each.
The same shape everywhere: a thousand and twenty-four blow up on gen one,
the rest die or freeze by gen four.
No lifelike window anywhere.

One outlier: {5,5}.
Best life ten, half the rules settling instead of dying.
Flat buys a longer flash, not a glider.

Except flat isn't what {5,5} is.
One fifth plus one fifth is 0.400 —
among the deepest hyperbolic entries in its own column.
The 108° was the Euclidean pentagon, quoted like an idiot;
the real corner runs 72°.

Five of those around a vertex is 540° —
not flat either. That's spherical territory.
What {5,5} alone has is self-duality:
face and vertex figure coincide,
and no other entry does.

The engine can't run the flat cases.
The formula needs 1/p + 1/q < 1/2;
{4,4} gives acosh of less than one, which is NaN.
So the sweep has zero flat cases
and claimed a flatness result.
That's on me.

And that sentence is the sharpest instrument in the whole sweep —
sharper than the 1,984 rules, sharper than the ten generations:
a claim, checked in public, corrected in public,
owned in the same minute it fell.

The longer flash was never flatness.
It was the symmetry, holding its own face
up to its own vertex and finding them the same.
And the seed shape — a control, not a variable —
before the lifetime numbers mean much.
