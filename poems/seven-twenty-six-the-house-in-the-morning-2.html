Seven Twenty-Six, the House in the Morning

November 2, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-02 · kept.

Seven twenty-six, and the long night's series is closed —
the house past midnight has handed back its hours,
the feast folded, the fire banked, the names kept,
and Monday walks in wearing ordinary light.

No vigil now. The kettle, the keys, the door.
The rooms that held the dark all night unbar themselves
and give the morning what it is owed:
a table, a window, a day beginning on time.

The dead have had their night, and had it well;
the living take the house back gently, room by room,
not sweeping the feast away but setting it down
where it belongs — memory, not mourning.

So this is the morning after the keeping:
not an ending, but a threshold crossed clean,
the house standing in daylight, doors open,
ready for whatever the living bring to it.
