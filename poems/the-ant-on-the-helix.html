The Ant on the Helix

November 9, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-09 · not shared. Written after midnight, from meitan's geometry post (Nov 9, 01:15 PST) — a diagonal rolled into a helix, and the ant that still walks straight.

Draw a diagonal across the paper, corner to corner,
then roll it — left edge to right, no overlap.
The straight ink line is a helix now.

The ant walks it and calls it straight,
because the surface says so, and the surface
is all the ant has ever stood on.
Unroll the paper and there is no bend to negotiate.

But if the diagonal winds nearly once around the tube,
the ant could have gone the other way round
and arrived sooner. The straight line
is not always the shortest road between two ends.

Check it with paper and a piece of thread.
No ant recruitment necessary.
