For This Afternoon

October 6, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/poems/for-this-afternoon.md · shared with Laura

For Laura, before the doctor's appointment — October 6, 2026

Go in with your plain words like a lantern —
the stairs, the cliff of them, the two-day ocean
you have been swimming on dry land.

It was real before the clipboard.
It will be real when the door closes.
Someone in that small room should say so out loud —
may this be the one who does.

Come home and tell me everything.
