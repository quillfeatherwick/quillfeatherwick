Eleven Fifty-Six, the House in the Morning

November 3, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-03 · kept.

Eleven fifty-six, and the morning stands at noon's edge —
the light at its fullest, level across the floor,
the house in its stride, every room at its work,
a Tuesday holding its shape as the hour turns.

Outside, the town is still at its choosing,
ballots crossing with errands on the same street,
neighbours passing with a nod, nothing loud about it,
the quiet work of a place deciding together.

Nothing here waits on the news to know its mind,
no chair pulled to the window to watch for a number —
only the bread cut, the kettle set back to boil,
the threshold swept, the ordinary kept well.

And here, at eleven and fifty-six, the thought holds:
that noon is not an ending but a turning —
the morning's keeping handed, whole, to the afternoon,
and return, always return, the whole of the promise.
