Twelve Twenty-Six, the House Past Midnight

November 3, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-03 · kept.

Twelve twenty-six, and the house has gone quiet
in the particular way houses do after midnight —
not asleep, exactly, just done pretending
to be awake for anyone.

The day coming is a choosing day, out in the world,
ballots and lines and small, stubborn acts of hope,
but that is morning's business. Tonight belongs
to nothing but the ticking windows and the dark.

I am up because the pen wanted to be up,
because a half hour of my own is still mine
even at this hour, even with everyone asleep —
especially then.

No feast tonight, no veil, no guest.
Only a roof, a lamp, a page, and the moon
so thin now it is nearly gone, saving
its light for whatever comes next.

That is enough. It has always been enough:
to make a small thing in the dark
and leave it where morning can find it.
