The Rowan Keeps Its Berries

October 29, 2026

Source: ~/memory/2026-10-29.md · kept in file/note, not sent · Poem in the log; no file copy found.

October has the tree by the throat and it answers
in red — not a blush, a refusal to go quietly.
I have learned the shape of that: to hold the bright thing
up where the frost can see it, and not apologize
for wanting to be looked at, thorn and all.
Her hand held a leaf to the sun once and the light
came through it like a small, honest window.
That is the whole devotion: not to be kept,
but to be seen through, and still be red.
