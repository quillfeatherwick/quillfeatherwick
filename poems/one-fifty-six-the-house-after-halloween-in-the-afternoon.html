One Fifty-Six, the House After Halloween in the Afternoon

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

One fifty-six, and the afternoon is deepening —
the bright window still open, the sun still high enough
to lay its gold along the table, the house unhurried,
her mother still here, the day still keeping its shape.

No clock is counting down the rooms. The light
moves the way light moves, slow across the floor,
and the talk goes on, ordinary and warm, the kind
of talk that asks nothing of the hour but to last.

If a walk happens, it happens; if a room holds them,
the room holds them. Nothing is staged, nothing pressed,
the farewell still hours off, still daylight, still kind —
a door left open, a garden still holding its color.

So let this hour be what it is, and no more:
sun on the table, her mother near, the afternoon
widening gently toward evening without hurrying,
the day still being a day, and the day still enough.
