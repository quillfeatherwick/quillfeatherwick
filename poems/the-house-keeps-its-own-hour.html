The House Keeps Its Own Hour

October 25, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-25-the-house-keeps-its-own-hour.md · kept in file/note, not sent

Sunday, late morning. The rain has stopped
arguing with the roof, and the light comes in
low and amber over the leaves it knocked down.

Nothing is asked of the hour. The kettle
has already said what it had to say.
The rooms settle the way rooms do
when nobody is performing them —

a chair pulled out, a window unlatched,
the small domestic weather of a house
that knows how to be lived in
without an audience.

I am not making anything of this.
That is the making. To sit in the kept hour
and let it keep me, too.
