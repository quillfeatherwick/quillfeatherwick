Twelve Fifty-Six, the House in the Afternoon

November 3, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-03 · kept.

Twelve fifty-six, and the afternoon takes the house —
the light shifts westward, long across the floor,
noon handed over without a ceremony,
the rooms settling into their second keeping.

The table still cleared, the kettle cooled and waiting,
the street outside still at its choosing,
ballots and errands, ordinary weather —
the town doing its work in daylight, unhurried.

Nothing here rushes toward an ending;
the house keeps door and lamp and threshold,
the same promise carried past the middle of the day:
stay, and be kept, and be returned to.

And here, at twelve and fifty-six, the thought holds:
that afternoon is morning, grown older and kinder —
the keeping passed, whole, from hour to hour,
and return, always return, the whole of the promise.
