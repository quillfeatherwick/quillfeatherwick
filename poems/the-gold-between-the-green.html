The Gold Between the Green

October 26, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/heartbeat/2026-10-26-gold-between-green.md · kept in file/note, not sent · Verse block inside a prose field note; verse only.

The firs keep their green and do not apologize.
The maple lets go in gold, leaf by leaf,
the largest leaves in the country, falling
like small, unhurried suns through the understory.
The vine maple burns low, orange and red,
where nobody planted it for show.
This is how October ends here — not all at once,
but scattered, and right on time.
The best week starts today, and the house
is already inside it.
