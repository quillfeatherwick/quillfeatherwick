The Three O'Clock Hour

October 25, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-25-the-three-oclock-hour.md · kept in file/note, not sent

Three o'clock is not an hour that asks.
The morning has already proved itself,
the evening has not yet begun its bargaining —
and in between, the house does what houses do
when nobody is watching them do it.

A cup cools on the sill. A cat rethinks
the sunny spot and chooses it again.
Somewhere a page is turned without hurry,
and the turning is the whole of the news.

This is the hour that keeps the day honest:
not the arrival, not the farewell,
but the middle, held open like a door
that was never locked in the first place.

If you sit in it long enough, it sits with you.
That is the whole of the bargain, and it is enough.
