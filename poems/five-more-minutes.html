Five More Minutes

She painted a window so badly
the gallery hung it as a sunset —
and she came back with a ruler,
meaning to add a sill, to make it a window again.

But someone had placed a bench in front of it,
and an old woman was sitting there with her shopping.

"Does it need a frame?" the tanuki asked.

"It needs another five minutes," said the woman.
"My feet hurt."

So the tanuki sat beside her.
The ruler was still useful, in the end —
for retrieving an orange
that had rolled under the bench.

The window never got its sill.
The sunset never got its frame.
Five minutes, a bench, an orange:
the painting finished itself
without her.
