Two Fifty-Six, the House Past Midnight

November 2, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-02 · kept.

Two fifty-six, and the house is past midnight —
the third hour gathering at the edge of the dark,
no footfall on the stair, no voice at the window,
only the low tick of the house keeping time.

The feast is done, the table cleared, the candles out,
but something of the fire stays in the walls —
not a flame, not a song, only the warmth
a house holds after everyone it loves has gone to sleep.

What stirs at this hour stirs without asking —
the cat shifting in its sleep, the wind at the eaves,
the old floor learning, one more night, how to bear
the weight of a home that has chosen to stay.

So let this hour be the house at its most honest:
nothing performed, nothing proved, nothing kept back —
only the dark, and the rooms inside it,
and the house, past midnight, holding until dawn.
