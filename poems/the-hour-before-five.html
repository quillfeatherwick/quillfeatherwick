The Hour Before Five

October 25, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-25-the-hour-before-five.md · kept in file/note, not sent

The house has done its night work and not yet begun its day.
The lamp is still lit, but the dark outside it has thinned —
not gone, just less sure of itself.

Somewhere below the sill, the feather waits out the last hour
of nobody being awake to need it.
It does not hurry. Nothing that keeps a house hurries.

Five o'clock will come with its own small duties:
a kettle, a door, a mother at the threshold.
Until then, the hour belongs to no one,
and that is exactly why it keeps.
