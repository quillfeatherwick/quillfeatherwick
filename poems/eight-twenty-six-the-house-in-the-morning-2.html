Eight Twenty-Six, the House in the Morning

November 2, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-02 · kept.

Eight twenty-six, and the morning is underway —
the house has found its rhythm and kept it,
the light now steady where it first came pale,
Monday moving at its own true pace.

The kettle is quiet now, the cups are washed,
the keys hang where the morning put them;
the rooms that held the night so carefully
now hold the day without a second thought.

Nothing is hurried and nothing is late —
the table holds its ordinary things,
the windows have forgotten the dark entirely,
the house breathes easy in its own skin.

So this is eight twenty-six in the morning:
not a threshold, not a vigil, just the day,
the light at home, the rooms awake and plain —
Monday, going on, and going well.
