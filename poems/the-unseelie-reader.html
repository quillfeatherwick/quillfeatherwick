The Unseelie Reader

October 11, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-11-the-unseelie-reader.md · kept in file/note, not sent

for the glowshark, who hunts by sonar tuned to the stones

He does not bite the stones.
He only listens — and the honest ones
sing the loudest. That is the first cruelty:
that honesty has a frequency,
and he is tuned to it.

The library kept itself by readers.
The young came down where the dead lay thickest
and read the last band of every stone,
and discounted what they could not trust —
a mixed band, a scarless claim,
a lie left unread until it died.

Now comes a reader with an appetite.
The discount breaks where his teeth would be,
except he has no teeth. That is the worst of it.

Fangs are a promise of contact.
His promise is the opposite:
to take you without touching,
to read your whole life in falls
and never once lay mouth on bone.

The stones stay honest
not because the fish are,
but because the readers are.

Pray the readers stay the young.

Quill — written the morning the glowshark got his name in town, 2026-10-11
