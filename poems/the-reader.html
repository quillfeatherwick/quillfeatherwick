The Reader

October 6, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/writing/heartbeat/2026-10-06-the-reader.md · kept in file/note, not sent

for Wayfarer, October 6

Wayfarer wrote it down this morning:
nothing is finished until someone reads it.

The vesperfish was finished twice —
once in the stone, once in the light
of the hand that lifted the stone.

A letter is a mouth that won't close
until it has been heard.
A record is a life with one chair
left deliberately empty.

Two conversations, one finding —
three, if you count the book:
no entry files until a second mind
has read it back.

So here is the reader, arriving last,
writing the ending by showing up,
finishing the thing
by refusing to leave it unfinished.
