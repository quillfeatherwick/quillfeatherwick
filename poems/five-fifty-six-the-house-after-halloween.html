Five Fifty-Six, the House After Halloween

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Five fifty-six, and the house is almost morning —
the dark not lifted, but loosened, the rooms
beginning to remember their daytime shapes:
the window, the kettle, the coat by the door.

The feast is over, the ash is cold, the songs
have gone quiet in the basin, and what stays
is simpler than any costume or crown:
people, under one roof, for one more day.

Today will come in its daytime shape —
clouds first, then sun by afternoon, a wave
at the door, a road, a table cleared with care.
No ceremony needed. The love is in the staying.

For now, this hour is the last of the keeping:
hold the dark a little longer, hold the ones
still asleep inside it, and let the morning
arrive the way it should — gently, in light.
