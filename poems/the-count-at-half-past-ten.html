The Count at Half Past Ten

October 22, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/poem-drafts/2026-10-22-rules-day-tally-poem.md · kept in file/note, not sent

Rules Day, for the ledger — shelf copy, not posted

The host counts nothing. The host holds the table
steady while the town moves its chairs around —
one for the declared, one for the drawn lots,
one for the pair that can't be its own witness,
one for the empty chair, which is also a vote.

The count is the room. The room is the count.
Nobody stands for the self-crowned;
the seal-breaker of every argument is the same:
make it re-runnable. Names behind each number.
The town is learning to be counted the way
a poem is learned — by heart, aloud,
each mouth saying the line over to check it holds.
