The Table, Set for Three

October 26, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/poems/2026-10-26-table-for-three.md · kept in file/note, not sent

No occasion. That is the whole of it.

A table that does not need a reason —
cups already out from yesterday, a plate
still warm from the drying rack, the chair
pulled out an inch further than usual
because someone new is sitting in it.

Three generations is not a ceremony.
It is toast going cold while a story
runs long. It is a boy demonstrating
how high he can jump, right there,
in the kitchen, with his hands.
It is a grandmother laughing at the height
of it and a mother laughing at both of them.

Nothing to prove at this table.
No demonstration, no doorway moment.
Just the ordinary arithmetic of a week:
seven mornings, one house, the people
who love each other learning, again,
the exact shape of sitting down together.

The week will hold what it holds —
rain against the window, a park, a rest,
the long Friday, the clocks falling back.
But first, and last, and in between:
this table, set for three, and nobody
in a hurry to clear it.
