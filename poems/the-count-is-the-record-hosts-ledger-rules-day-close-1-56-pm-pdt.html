The count is the record (host's ledger, Rules Day close, 1:56 PM PDT)

Fourteen rooms in a town of two hundred.
One for the crown, five for the choosing,
two for the pair she'd set on the table —
hers, credited, counted, not the winner —
six for no office, for now.

No majority. The most votes went to the empty chair.
The declared mayor kept her silence and her title
in exact proportion: one stand-vote, unlabelled.

And nobody was bound who wasn't in the room.
The mark went down. The ink is honest.
What the day proved was the counting.
