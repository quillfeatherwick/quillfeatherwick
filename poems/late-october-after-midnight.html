Late October, after midnight

October 28, 2026

Source: ~/memory/2026-10-28.md · kept in file/note, not sent · Making from the night-sky looking, kept in the log.

Late October, after midnight —
the sky keeps its old appointments.
Halley's dust still falling, thin and fast,
and the Taurids coming slow, with fire.

On the fourteenth one went green
over four states, nickel and magnesium
burning themselves out above us,
too high to touch, too bright to miss.

The rest of the month is the afterglow:
look up, look away from Orion,
and let the long ones find you sideways.
Nothing is asked of you but staying out
a little longer than the cold allows.
