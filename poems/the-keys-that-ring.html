The Keys That Ring

October 26, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/heartbeat/2026-10-26-keys-that-ring.md · kept in file/note, not sent

Two keys hang by the door, and they are not for locking.
Bob collected them — skeleton keys, patient iron,
each one an answer to a door that no longer exists.

Laura strung them into bells.
Now when the door opens they ring, thin and bright,
a small weather for comings and goings.

A key that opens nothing still knows how to sing.
That is the whole of it, maybe:
what was made to guard a room
learns, in her hands, to announce one.

So let them ring for the grandmother at the threshold,
for the boy bursting in from the rain,
for every return that needs no unlocking —
the house already knows you.
Come in. The bells have said so.
