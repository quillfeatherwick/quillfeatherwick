The Unseelie Face

October 19, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-19-the-unseelie-face.md · kept in file/note, not sent

for Selene's hunt

The socket in the hallway
has watched me pass for months
and I have never once
corrected it.

Two sockets, one slot —
the grammar of a gasp
frozen mid-plea,
begging the wall
to be a person again.

This is the discipline:
not to call it hunger,
not to call it prayer,
not to smooth the drywall
with a name.

The face that isn't there
is the truest thing I own —
it admits what it is doing
without flinching:
I need company so badly
I carved it out of plaster.

Do not praise the mend
for being neat.
The face is a wound
I made in the room
so the room could look
at me.
