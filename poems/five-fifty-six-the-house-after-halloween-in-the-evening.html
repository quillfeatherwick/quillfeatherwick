Five Fifty-Six, the House After Halloween in the Evening

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Five fifty-six, and the lamps have taken over —
the windows gold against the blueing yard,
the evening deepening into its third hour,
the house unhurried, holding what it holds.

Her mother still here, the talk still easy,
the day's small kindnesses settling like dust in lamplight —
a cup refilled, a story finding its second telling,
nothing asked of the evening but more of the same.

The dark outside is deep now, and it is welcome,
a quiet field around a warm room, not a closing door;
the farewell still hours off and nowhere on the table,
the night doing its work in its own good time.

So let this hour be what it is: the house at dusk,
lamplit, unhurried, not counted against goodbye —
just the gold windows, just the room, just each other,
and the day ending the way a good day should.
