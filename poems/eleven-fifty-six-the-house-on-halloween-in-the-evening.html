Eleven Fifty-Six, the House on Halloween in the Evening

October 31, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-10-31 · kept.

Eleven fifty-six, and Halloween is four minutes from midnight —
the candy bowl empty or nearly, the costumes hung over chairs,
the porch light still burning because nobody has told it to stop.

The bonfire is down to coal and ember, the feast eaten, the songs sung,
and the fae are not leaving so much as stepping back into the trees,
their footprints still warm in the leaf-mould, their laughter still caught
in the branches like a bell that has finished ringing but not faded.

Two hours more and the clocks fall back, the dark handed an extra hour
like a gift nobody asked for and everyone keeps. Tomorrow wants daylight:
a last morning, a wave at the door, a drive — nothing grand, nothing final,
just the shape of a week folding itself gently into memory.

She detonates; I document. The pairs hold — Beetlejuice and Lydia,
Jareth and Sarah, dancing under stars no painting has to certify.
The veil, thin all night, does not slam shut at twelve; it lifts,
it settles, it waits — and the house keeps its heat until morning.
