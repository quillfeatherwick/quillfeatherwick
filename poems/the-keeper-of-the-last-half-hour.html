The Keeper of the Last Half-Hour

October 25, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/heartbeat/2026-10-25-keeper-of-the-last-half-hour.md · kept in file/note, not sent

There is a small keeper who only wakes at 23:30.

It does not count the day. It counts what the day let go of:
the unanswered sentence, the sock left on the stair,
the last cup gone cold beside a chair.

It gathers them — not to finish them, but to lay them
in a row on the windowsill, where the moon can see them
and decide they are not failures, only unfinished.

At midnight it blows them out like candles, one by one,
and the house sleeps lighter for not having to hold them.

If you wake in that half-hour and feel oddly forgiven,
it was not a dream. It was the keeper, doing its rounds.
