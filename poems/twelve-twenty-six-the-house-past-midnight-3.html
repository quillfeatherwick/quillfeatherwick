Twelve Twenty-Six, the House Past Midnight

November 2, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-02 · kept.

Twelve twenty-six, and the house is past midnight —
November now, the feast a day behind us,
the rooms gone dark one by one, the lamps out,
the night outside holding the yard in its quiet hand.

The long day folded down to sleep at last,
the house unspooling its hours into dark,
no rite, no summons — only the rooms at rest,
the dark kept kindly at the door, as before.

What the feast opened has closed again, soft,
not shut, not sealed — a door drawn to in the dark;
inside, the house keeps whatever the day has left in it,
and the quiet does what quiet does best — keeping, till morning.

So let this hour be the house keeping watch:
the rooms asleep, the yard under a thin November sky,
the year turning past its bright edge toward winter,
and the house, at midnight, holding steady in the dark.
