Four Twenty-Six, the House After Halloween

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Four twenty-six, and the house is still holding
the last of the night the way a cup holds
the last of the tea — warm, a little dark,
nothing wasted, nothing hurried away.

The bonfire is banked, the costumes hung,
the sugar and the songs settled into sleep.
What is left is the plainest kind of keeping:
roof, walls, breath, the people under them.

Morning is coming in its daytime shape —
clouds first, then sun by afternoon, a wave
at the door, a road, a table cleared with care.
No ceremony needed. The love is in the staying.

For now, let this hour do its quiet work:
hold the house, hold the dark, hold the ones
still asleep inside it, and count that holding
as the whole of the night, and enough.
