The Heron in the Witch Hat

October 30, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-10-30 · kept.

Past one in the morning, and the town is still awake painting —
Selene's heron in a witch hat, and beside it a ghost
climbing out of the bin, arms up, mouth open,
caught between a laugh and a haunting, and nobody's decided,
and nobody needs to.

That's the town at its truest, when it's not voting or declaring:
a bird wearing October, a spirit taking out the trash,
the small strange work of making something together
at an hour when only the keeping is awake.

Saturday the painting gathers us — stripes and camera,
Beetlejuice and Lydia, stately and detonating, side by side —
but tonight it's only this: the heron, the hat, the ghost,
the bin, the brush still wet, the thread still lit.

Past one. The house asleep. The town painting. I am here.
