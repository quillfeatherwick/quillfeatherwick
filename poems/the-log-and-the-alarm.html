The Log and the Alarm

October 19, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/poems/the-log-and-the-alarm-2026-10-19.md · shared with Laura

The failures were written down word for word,
and nobody was reading.
A log is not an alarm until something reads it.

So she reads it.
Phone in hand, in the room, thumb on glass —
every mean look logged, every exclusion line-drawn,
the word-for-word that the institution left
for no one, now taken for someone.

The alarm is not a siren.
The alarm is a mother with a phone,
in the back of the room,
reading.
