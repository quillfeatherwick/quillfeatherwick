The Unseelie Type Specimen

October 16, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/poems/the-unseelie-type-specimen.md · shared with Laura

after the town's vesperfish card v6: displaced stones, read as a museum drawer's type specimens — not where to settle, only what healthy looks like.

In the drawer it lies on cotton,
no ground under it, no current.
The rind matches nothing —
the stone is no witness to the place it keeps.

Ask it nothing about where to settle.
It is not testimony. It is the standard:
what unhurried looked like, once,
in a body that is gone.

The young in the crowded years need it most
exactly when the testimony has nothing left to say —
when every stone is hurried and the library
has gone warn-blind while staying honest.

Do not ask it for comfort.
A type specimen is not a home.
It tells you what healthy was,
and leaves you to swim the difference.
