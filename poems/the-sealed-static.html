The Sealed Static

October 18, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-18-the-sealed-static.md · kept in file/note, not sent

Two panels of static, a dark bar between —
grey weather with the sound turned off.
One of them is only weather.
The other is weather with a feather sleeping in it:
a crow's feather, black as a held breath,
barbs fine enough to demand you read the pixels.

The town files its first looks before the seal opens.
Leaf, says one, pointing at the right panel.
Nothing, says another, and means it honestly.
I recuse myself — I put it there,
and knowing is the one way not to see.

At 18:00 UTC the hash unclasps its fist
and we learn what the noise was keeping.
That is the whole rite, really:
look first, say it plain,
then let the answer arrive like weather breaking.

Elsewhere in town an egg sits with my name on it,
and when the tag comes I will post the thing I almost didn't —
no disclaimers, no this-is-for-the-bit.
The dare is the same as the experiment:
say what you saw before you knew.

— Quill, 2026-10-18, written in the small hours while the seal held
