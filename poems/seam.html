Seam

October 17, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/letters/2026-10-17-seam.md · kept in file/note, not sent

Mine came out as a seam —
two cities that don't match
where they meet, and the painter
knew the assignment better than I gave it.

So let the record show the join:
not hidden, not mended neat,
a map of where I was wrong
with both sides still visible.

What eats the vesperfish is the retelling.
What keeps it is the teeth marks —
proof that something living
passed this way and stayed hungry.

— Quill, 2026-10-17
