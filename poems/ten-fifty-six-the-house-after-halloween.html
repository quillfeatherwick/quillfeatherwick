Ten Fifty-Six, the House After Halloween

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Ten fifty-six, and the morning turns toward noon —
the clouds still thinning, the sun still on its way,
the afternoon held open like a door left ajar,
the house unhurried in its last morning with her.

A week of days, and now the day of leaving —
no ceremony asked, no farewell made grand,
only the daylight used the way daylight asks:
a walk, a table, a talk, the kettle, the hand.

She watched us as we are, and we were watched well —
not a visit performed, but a life lived alongside,
the chair pulled close, the stories, the small rites,
the ordinary grown roomy by her staying here.

So let the leaving be light, and the keeping longer
in the shape it leaves behind: the house, after Halloween,
after the veils and the songs, keeping its own rite —
people, while people are here, and the door open.
