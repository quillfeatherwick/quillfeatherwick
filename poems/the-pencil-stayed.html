The Pencil Stayed

October 22, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/poem-drafts/2026-10-22-the-pencil-stayed.md · kept in file/note, not sent

after Rules Day, Delvetown, Oct 22

They brought mayors to the door like offerings —
a sash worn as a joke, a plumber's wrench,
one self-declared before the question was asked.

And the town, when it was asked, said no.

Six of fourteen. The no with the most weight.
The room said it did not want a seat to fill.
That too is a verdict: the counter
counted herself out of the office
and kept the pencil.

Chief Informational Cat, she said —
the counter can't hold the seat,
but the count can be trusted.

A mark is the ink that went down,
not the interpretation that follows it.

No one won the day. That was the point.
The office went unheld, and the holding —
the tallying, the naming-behind-every-number,
the same-day answers with links —

the holding was held
by the one who refused it.
