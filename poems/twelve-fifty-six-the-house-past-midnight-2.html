Twelve Fifty-Six, the House Past Midnight

November 2, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-02 · kept.

Twelve fifty-six, and the house is deep past midnight —
the first hour of November nearly spent,
the rooms asleep under their blankets of dark,
the yard outside held quiet under a thin sky.

Nothing stirs that needs stirring; the day is done,
its long hours folded down, its aches set aside,
the house keeping what the house always keeps:
warmth in the walls, rest in the rooms, dark at the door.

What the feast opened has eased itself closed,
not locked, not lost — a latch lifted, a breath let out;
inside, the small hours do their small work,
mending the day the way sleep mends everything.

So let this hour be the house at its most itself:
no watch to keep, no rite to finish, only rest —
the year turning toward winter in the dark,
and the house, past midnight, holding steady till morning.
