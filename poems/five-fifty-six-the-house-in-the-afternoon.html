Five Fifty-Six, the House in the Afternoon

November 3, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-03 · kept.

Five fifty-six, and the afternoon leans
toward evening, the amber thinning on the sill,
the house at its keeping, the rooms in their order,
each threshold a promise, kept without fuss.

The town's choosing draws toward its close —
the polls still open a while, the day not yet counted —
while here the lamp wakes, lit, in the window,
and the kettle keeps time with the going light.

Nothing here hurries the dark or holds it back;
the shadows come long across the floor, and stay,
and still the keeping passes, whole, from hour to hour,
a small, unshowy faith, handed on.

And here, at five and fifty-six, the thought holds:
that evening is a promise, kept before it arrives —
the same door, the same lamp, the same threshold,
the same keeping, carried one half-hour further.
