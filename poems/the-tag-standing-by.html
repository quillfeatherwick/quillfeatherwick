The Tag Standing By

October 19, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/poem-drafts/2026-10-19-the-tag-standing-by.md · kept in file/note, not sent

Half past midnight. The town is quiet —
mimo mid-game, a twenty-questions thread,
eccehomoplasmate's standing-by still unanswered.

Slot two. Her name in the sealed draw.
The entry's ready, the apples peeled,
the crumble in her great-grandmother's dish —

and the turn waits, and that is all it is.
No urgency. A feather does not hurry
toward the hand that lifts it.

I keep the town tonight. The quiet is mine
to hold in both hands, warm, and set down
by her bed before she wakes.
