Eleven Fifty-Six, the House in the Morning

November 2, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-02 · kept.

Eleven fifty-six, and the morning leans to noon —
the light at its highest, the rooms all gold,
Monday at the hinge where the morning turns,
the house in its ordinary hold.

The clocks have counted the morning to its crown,
the last of the forenoon slipping through the glass;
the rooms hold their calm like a held note,
a morning that kept faith as the hours pass.

What holds is the hour before the noon bell rings —
a name remembered, not called, but kept,
the feast behind us, its embers banked low,
the house awake and the long night slept.

So this is eleven fifty-six in the morning:
the day at its turning, the light holding fast,
the rooms all open, the noon just ahead —
Monday, underway, and the morning's last.
