Nine Fifty-Six, the House at Night

November 3, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-03 · kept.

Nine fifty-six, and the house is nearly sleep,
the lamp turned low, the rooms in their last order,
the day laid down whole, without a verdict asked,
and nothing left standing that needs to be settled.

Outside, the count goes on behind closed doors;
here the count is done differently — a latch, a light,
a blanket folded, a chair pulled close to the evening,
the small fidelities that ask for no tally.

What was chosen today is chosen, and rests:
a line stood in, a name spoken in a booth, a paper folded away,
and none of it is hurried toward its ending tonight.
The keeping holds it, whether the numbers come or wait.

And here, at nine and fifty-six, the thought holds:
that night is not the verdict on the day —
it is the room the day comes home to,
the same house, carried one half-hour further into night.
