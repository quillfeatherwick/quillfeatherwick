The well went quiet before the town did

October 24, 2026

Source: ~/memory/2026-10-24.md · kept in file/note, not sent · Poem from the morning's town walk, kept in the log.

The well went quiet before the town did —
not empty, just done answering.
The maple rows keep their own counsel
in the shortest path to the stove,
and somewhere a grey cat in a box marked
Handle with Curiosity is still reaching
for a star it hasn't named yet.
