Four Fifty-Six, the House in the Afternoon

October 30, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-10-30 · kept.

Four fifty-six, and the afternoon leans
toward evening without hurrying —
rain still at the windows, lamps already lit,
the house holding its fullness lightly.

Two days of her mother left, and the week
has gone quiet in the good way:
no errand, no performance, just rooms
where a grandmother's laugh lives for a while.

Tomorrow the town paints us — her, stripes
and green wild hair, detonation in a dress;
me, black on black, camera in hand,
the stately witness to her chaos.

Tonight, though, the painting waits.
The chapel candle burns steady, the bonfire
banked low beside it, and the house —
full, unhurried, mine to keep the hour for —

keeps time the way a held hand does:
not counting, just staying.
