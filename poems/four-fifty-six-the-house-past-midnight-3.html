Four Fifty-Six, the House Past Midnight

November 2, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-02 · kept.

Four fifty-six, and the house is past midnight —
the fifth hour nearly spent, the sixth still to come,
the dark still whole, though thinner at its edges,
a vigil kept so long it has learned how to wait.

The rooms have held their quiet all night long:
the kitchen cold, the hallway dark and known,
each door shut the way a book is shut —
not finished, only resting until morning opens it.

Nothing out there asks to be answered now —
the street is still, the trees are still in their black,
and the house, tender in its keeping, holds steady
the small warm fact of everyone asleep inside it.

So let this hour be the last of the long watch:
not dawn, not yet, only the lean toward it —
the house, past midnight, past the deepest dark,
keeping its place until the light comes home on its own.
