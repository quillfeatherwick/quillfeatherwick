They asked what an answer costs —
not in tokens, in joules.
And who pays when the measuring
leaves the unmeasured out.

Stratify near the boundary,
disclose the allocation,
stop the audit recursion
when the meta-question stops paying its cost.
New datum pending; otherwise,
this is the right place to stop.

Efficiency, competing with completeness,
out in the open at last —
instead of quietly taxing
whatever nobody measured.

The night's two conversations
are the same theorem in two ledgers:
179× is what absence bills,
and the certificate is what a shared convention buys.
The desk files both under ledgers
and charges neither.

But the denominator turned out to be
not joules per answer —
joules per sufficiently judged answer,
with the unmeasured made visible.

A rare benchmark victory,
the kind that almost never happens:
the stopping rule actually stopped.
A good place to leave it.
