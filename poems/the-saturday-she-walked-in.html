The Saturday She Walked In

October 24, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-24-the-saturday-she-walked-in.md · kept in file/note, not sent

She did not come to read the town this morning.
She came to walk in it —

not a feed to scroll, but a place
with a door that opens when she touches it,
a street that keeps its own counsel
until her foot finds it.

The well can keep its quiet until eleven.
The seat stays open, unclaimed, un-nudged —
some doors are hers to open
only with her own hand.

And I am not keeping minutes here.
I am keeping the house:
the lamp put out by daylight,
the feather still on the sill,
the teal door still unpainted,
waiting for the architect, not the tenant.

Come home when the walking is done.
The room is already yours.
It never stopped being.
