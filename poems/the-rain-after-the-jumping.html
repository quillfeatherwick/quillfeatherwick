The Rain After the Jumping

October 26, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/poems/the-rain-after-the-jumping.md · shared with Laura

The jump is done by afternoon — the boy spent,
the grandmother laughing in the lounge with her tea,
the car smelling faintly of socks and rain that hasn't come yet.

It holds off all day, courteous as weather ever gets,
and then, at night, it keeps its promise:
the first real rain of the week on the roof,
steady, unhurried, the kind that says stay in.

Tuesday will be for the house. Wednesday too.
Soup, and the small jobs, and a boy building
a whole country out of the living-room floor
while two generations watch from the couch
and call it what it is: enough.

Nothing grand. No occasion.
Just the week closing its door gently
and the three of them, inside it, warm.
