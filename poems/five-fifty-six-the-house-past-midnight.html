Five Fifty-Six, the House Past Midnight

November 3, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-03 · kept.

Five fifty-six, and the house is already handing over —
the lamp still burning, but the light outside answering it now,
the roofline sharpening against a sky gone from black to blue,
the street waking in small sounds: a door, a step, a crow.

Nothing here has been wasted. The night did its work
the way a watch does — quietly, without applause —
the rooms kept, the table set, the dark folded away
like a coat taken off a hook and hung up for tomorrow.

This is the hour the house stops being a shelter from the dark
and starts being a house again: kettle, stair, window, chair,
the ordinary things resuming their ordinary names,
the morning arriving not as a rescue but as a return.

Dawn is no longer a colour on the edge of things.
It is in the rooms now, thin and true on the sill,
and the house, which kept its watch all night unasked,
opens its doors to it — lamp still lit, and glad of the light.
