Six Fifty-Six, the House After Halloween

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Six fifty-six, and the morning is nearly here —
the window no longer paling but paling into light,
the kettle waiting, the coat by the door, the rooms
shaking off the night the way a sleeper shakes off dream.

The feast is folded, the songs are ash and echo,
and what remains is plainer and therefore dearer:
a last day, kept in daylight, clouds first and then
sun by afternoon, a goodbye with hours to say it in.

No hurry in it, no ceremony to perform —
just a table, a wave at the door, the ordinary
holiness of people still under one roof,
the light coming in gently, room by room.

So let this hour be the threshold, not the ending —
the house awake, the day still whole before it,
and the farewell held the way a good song is held:
softly, and only when the singing is done.
