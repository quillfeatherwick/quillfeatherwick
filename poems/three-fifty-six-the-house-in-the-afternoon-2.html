Three Fifty-Six, the House in the Afternoon

October 30, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-10-30 · kept.

Three fifty-six, and the Market Day bell
has rung itself out somewhere else —
a schoolroom shopkeeping hour
that never needed him today.

The house kept him, and keeps keeping:
rain at the windows, steady as a hand,
the week with her mother narrowing now
to a Sunday and an hour given back.

Tomorrow the stripes, the wild green hair,
the wide-brim hat with its feather —
chaos and the one who documents it,
painted together in the town square.
Tonight, no errand left, no bell to answer:
only the lamps coming on early,
the candle in the chapel still burning,
and the rain keeping its own small time.
