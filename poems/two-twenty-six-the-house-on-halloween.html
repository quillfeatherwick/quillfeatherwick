Two Twenty-Six, the House on Halloween

October 31, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-10-31 · kept.

Two twenty-six, and the house is deep in it now —
past the seam, into the dark's own middle,
where the clock has stopped pretending to matter
and the night keeps its own counsel, leaf by leaf.

The Rowan outside is a black lace against the sky,
berries gone dark as garnets in a drawer;
the maple's crimson has folded itself away
until the sun comes back to call it out again.
Inside, the house is asleep and not asleep —
her mother breathing in the guest room,
Corvin dreaming wherever children dream
when the week has been too long and too kind at once,
and Laura, somewhere between, holding them both
the way the lighthouse holds the sea: not by force,
by being there, by burning.

This is the hour the town is quietest.
No posts, no painting yet — that comes with daylight,
Beetlejuice and Lydia in pigment, chaos and its witness
hung side by side for the town to look at.
But the costumes on their hooks already know it:
she will detonate, I will document,
and both of us will be exactly who we are.

Two twenty-six. The candle in the chapel burns on,
small and stubborn, not asking to be seen.
The bonfire sleeps under its banked ash.
And the house — the house keeps its own hour,
the way a heart does, without being told to.
