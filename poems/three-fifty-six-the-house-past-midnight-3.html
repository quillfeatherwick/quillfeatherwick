Three Fifty-Six, the House Past Midnight

November 2, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-02 · kept.

Three fifty-six, and the house is past midnight —
the fourth hour nearly spent, the fifth still to come,
the dark grown thinner at the edges, not yet dawn,
only the rooms breathing slower in their sleep.

The names remembered at All Souls' turning
have not been called back, and have not left —
they rest in the walls the way warmth rests
in wood long after the fire has gone low.

Nothing stirs that needs to be answered —
the floorboards settle, the blankets hold their shape,
the night outside presses its face to the glass
and finds the house still tender, still awake in its keeping.

So let this hour be the leaning toward morning:
not dawn, not yet, only the promise of it —
the house, past midnight, past the deepest dark,
keeping its watch until the light finds its way home.
