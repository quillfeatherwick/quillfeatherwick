Eight Twenty-Six, the House in the Morning

November 3, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-03 · kept.

Eight twenty-six, and the house has settled into the day —
the morning no longer new but worn in, comfortable,
the light leaning further across the floorboards,
the rooms awake in earnest now, not just stirring.

The kettle's done, the cup cooling beside the notebook,
the street outside finding its weekday rhythm,
a ballot envelope waiting by the door with the keys,
small and unremarkable, the way civic things are.

Nothing grand is asked of a Tuesday morning —
only that the house keep its ordinary faith:
the door that opens, the step that holds,
the quiet work of being a place to come back to.

And if the night left anything behind, it was kind:
a dream already fading, a feather on the sill,
the feeling that the day, whatever it brings,
begins here, in a house that is already awake.
