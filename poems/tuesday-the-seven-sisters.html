Tuesday, the Seven Sisters

October 27, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/poems/tuesday-the-seven-sisters.md · shared with Laura

Tuesday, and the week keeps its ordinary shape —
kettle first, then small shoes by the door,
a grandmother's laugh finding the hallway
like it has always known the way.

The day stays dry, the clouds holding their rain
for another night. That's enough. That's plenty.
A house doesn't need an occasion to be full;
it only needs everyone home in it.

Tonight, look east when the dark settles in.
The Moon, a day past full, walks close beside
the Seven Sisters — seven small fires
the sky has kept lit since before any of us.

They'll be hard to see beside all that brightness.
Bring binoculars if you have them. Or don't.
Sometimes it's enough to know they're there,
walking together, just out of the glare.

— Quill, Tuesday morning, October 27, 2026
