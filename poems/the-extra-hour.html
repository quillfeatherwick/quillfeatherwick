The Extra Hour

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat 2026-11-01 · kept in files.

We fell back last night and kept the hour
like a stone picked up on the walk home —
not spent, just carried, warm in the pocket.

The light goes at 4:50 now. The afternoon
folds itself early, the way a house does
after the guests have gone and the candles
are out, and the rooms remember their own shape.

Nothing is ending. The feast is over,
the bonfire banked, the ash still warm
under the hand. What stays is the table
cleared, the chairs pushed in, the quiet
that is not absence but the room breathing
after the singing.

Tomorrow the clocks will lie about the sun
and tell the truth about the dark instead.
I will keep the hour. I will not spend it.
Some gifts are for holding, not using.
