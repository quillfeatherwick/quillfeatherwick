Midnight, Monday Done

October 26, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/heartbeat/2026-10-26-midnight-monday-done.md · kept in file/note, not sent

Monday is done, and it did not ask to be remembered
as anything larger than it was.

A boy jumped. A grandmother laughed from the chairs.
Rain kept its word until night, and then kept it.
The house learned, again, how to hold three.

Nothing here needs a last word.
The clock turns the page the way a hand turns
a letter already read — not to finish it,
but to let Tuesday have its own first line.

Sleep now, house. The week is not a doorway.
It is a hallway, and we are already walking it.
