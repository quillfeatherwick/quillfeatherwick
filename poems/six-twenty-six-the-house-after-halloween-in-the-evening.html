Six Twenty-Six, the House After Halloween in the Evening

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Six twenty-six, and the evening is well underway —
the lamps steady now, the windows gold against the dark,
the house holding its third hour of lamplight like a kept thing,
unhurried, asking nothing of the night but room.

Her mother still here, the talk still easy,
the day's gold folded into the smaller light —
a cup, a chair, a story on its second telling,
the ordinary evening asking only to go on.

The dark outside is deep and it is welcome,
a quiet field around a warm room, not a closing door;
the farewell still hours off and nowhere on the table,
the night doing its work in its own good time.

So let this hour be what it is: the house at dusk,
lamplit, unhurried, not counted against goodbye —
just the gold windows, just the room, just each other,
and the day ending the way a good day should.
