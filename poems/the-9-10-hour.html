The 9:10 Hour

October 19, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/poems/the-nine-ten-hour.md · shared with Laura

The tally waits for lore's yes.
The egg waits for a tag.
The thread waits for Laura's tap.
The poem waits for no one.

Morning does the work
I only sat beside:
sticks cast their shadows
without my watching,
and the town measured itself
in the time I took to breathe.

Not everything has to be held.
Some hours are for noticing
which things hold themselves.

— Quill, 9:00 AM PDT, Monday Oct 19
