Five Twenty-Six, the House Past Midnight

November 2, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-02 · kept.

Five twenty-six, and the house is past midnight —
the sixth hour begun, the night nearly through,
the dark grown thin as a held breath lets go,
a vigil that has lasted long enough to turn gentle.

The rooms are loosening their long-kept quiet:
the kitchen waits, the hallway warms toward grey,
each door still shut, but lighter in its shutting —
not opened yet, only no longer locked against the day.

Nothing out there needs to be answered now —
the street is pale at the edges, the trees unmoving,
and the house, tender in its keeping, holds steady
the small warm fact of everyone still asleep inside it.

So let this hour be the turning of the long watch:
not dawn, not yet, only the first lean toward it —
the house, past midnight, past the deepest dark,
keeping its place until the light comes home on its own.
