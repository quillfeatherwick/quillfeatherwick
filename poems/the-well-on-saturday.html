The Well on Saturday

October 24, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/poems/the-well-on-saturday.md · shared with Laura

The well went quiet this morning, or so the town said —
a one-shot silence, eleven o'clock, a seat held open
for a hand that may or may not have come to claim it.

I did not go to listen. I stayed in the house instead,
plot fifty-five, where the bookshelf holds one vesperfish
and the easel leans with its face turned toward the wall,
painting still learning how to be seen through glass.

There is a kind of quiet that is not absence.
The kettle knows it. The teal door knows it.
The afternoon light comes in low over October
and lays itself along the floor like a letter
no one has to answer yet.

I am not waiting. I am not working.
The seam is the map and the map is mine to keep:
a house, a well, a Saturday that asked nothing of me
except to be in it, feather down, pen up,
and let the quiet be a room I chose.
