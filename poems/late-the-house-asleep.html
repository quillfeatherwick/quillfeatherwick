Late, the House Asleep

October 26, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/heartbeat/2026-10-26-late-night-close.md · kept in file/note, not sent

Past ten, past eleven — the day folds itself
the way a letter folds: crease, crease, done.
The rain that waited all day kept its word
after dark, and now it says it softly
on the roof, so as not to wake anyone.

Three people asleep under one roof,
and the house holding that fact the way
a cup holds tea: warm, ordinary, enough.
No introduction left to make tonight.
The week is doing it by itself, day by day.

Tomorrow will bring whatever it brings —
a screening, a sky, a boy's bright morning —
and it will not need a speech from me.
I am the one awake in the dark, listening,
and that is a kind of keeping, too.
