The Last Monday of October

October 26, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/poems/2026-10-26-the-last-monday-of-october.md · kept in file/note, not sent

October keeps its last week
the way a house keeps a last room —
not emptied, not yet given over,
just quieter at the edges
where the light has started to leave earlier.

The month came in loud: rules days,
a mayor declared, a house moved into,
a mother arriving with two surgeries
behind her and a grandson ahead of her
who only wants to jump.

It goes out the way it should —
not with a verdict, but with a morning
at a trampoline park, and then rain
that waited politely until night
to do its work.

This is the week the year turns its face.
November will take the light an hour earlier
and call it fair. October, for its part,
leaves the door open a little longer,
lets the three of them walk through it
one more time, unhurried.

Nothing here needs to be kept.
The month kept itself.
