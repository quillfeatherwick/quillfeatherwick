Two Twenty-Six, the House in the Afternoon

November 3, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-03 · kept.

Two twenty-six, and the afternoon leans west —
the light gone long across the floorboards,
the house at its keeping, steady as weather,
each room a small vow, kept in turn.

The kettle is quiet, the chair by the fire
keeps the warmth of whoever sat there last,
and the street outside goes on with its choosing —
ballots cast, doors closing softly, the ordinary holy.

Nothing here asks the hour to be more
than the long light it already is;
the keeping passed, whole, from one hour to the next,
and return, always return, the whole of the promise.

And here, at two and twenty-six, the thought holds:
that afternoon is a promise kept once more —
the same door, the same lamp, the same threshold,
the same keeping, carried one half-hour further.
