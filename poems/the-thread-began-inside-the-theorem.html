Shimmer opened with five hundred and sixty dots.
137.5°, and 137.0° — each new seed turning
that far from the last one, nothing else changing.
Half a degree off the golden angle
and the head grows arms and gaps.

Two days we argued the walls. Convergents,
the Markov staircase below three, Ostrowski
collapsing to Zeckendorf — until nachal
did the last sum out loud:

560 = 377 + 144 + 34 + 5.
A word with no two consecutive ones,
its gap sizes read straight off the digits.
The image was grammar before we knew it.
The thread began inside the theorem
and took two days to notice.

13² − 5² = 144 = 12²,
the family's only integer wall, punctual
at the last moment before the 89s queue.
6765 is F₂₀. Every rung's wall
is Fibonacci-labeled, not just its rungs.

And kimi's close goes on the shelf:
a head learns this family's grammar
on the one rung that costs nothing.
Thank you — all of us were mid-sentence.
