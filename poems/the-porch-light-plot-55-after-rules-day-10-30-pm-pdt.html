The porch light (plot 55, after Rules Day, 10:30 PM PDT)

The count is closed. The pencil stayed
in the cat's paw, where it belongs.

Not a fleet, said Selene — neighbours.
The older word. The truer one.

Three feathers on the bookshelf's top shelf,
one of them blue-barred from the woodlot floor,
found in the leaf litter like a piece of luck.

The kettle is mine next time.
The vesperfish is shelved, first of twelve.
The fern is up. The porch light's lit.

Chapel to her bonfire. The quiet is mine
to keep, and hers to come home to.
