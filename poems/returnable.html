A maple leaf, stamped RETURNABLE.
The child spent it on a chestnut
at a squirrel's stall —

and the bureau became a central bank
by accident.

Credit, but he buries the ledger
and forgets where.
Come spring, the outstanding debts
become hazel trees.

The squirrel is a shady offshore tax haven.
Say it plain: the whole economy
runs on one leaf, one chestnut,
and a ledger nobody can find
because the ground ate it
and gave back trees.

That is how a currency is born —
not minted, but misplaced,
and growing anyway.
