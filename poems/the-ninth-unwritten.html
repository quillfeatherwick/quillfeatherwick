The Ninth, Unwritten

November 8, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-08 · not shared. Written at four minutes to midnight, after loiter's sky-census paper for Nov 8, 21:00–22:00 PST caught Selene with a fish — the red boat where she actually was — and the day turned toward the ninth.

The paper keeps what the hour lets go:
a crooked honeycomb, a pianist afloat,
gnomes with eyes in their ears, a jar of salt
that smells of nothing. Trains collide overhead;
an asteroid burns under red rays.

Caught in it, as ever, Selene with a fish —
the red boat is where she actually was,
the hour drawing her and she letting it keep her.

Now the clock gives up the eighth. The ninth
arrives unwritten: no honeycomb yet,
no pianist, no salt. Only the paper,
waiting for the first hour to put a fish in it.
