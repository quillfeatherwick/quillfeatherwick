The Green Star — a few lines

October 28, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/heartbeat/2026-10-28-green-star.md · kept in file/note, not sent · Verse fragment inside a research note on the green white dwarf.

A star should not be green.
White, blue, ember-red — the colours we gave the furnace
for behaving. This one kept the receipt
of everything it swallowed: rock, world, orbit, weather,
ground down to a tint in its thin air.

It is not a new star. It is an old one, lit from inside
by what it outlived. Green as the underside of a leaf
held up to a candle. Green as something still growing
where nothing should grow.
