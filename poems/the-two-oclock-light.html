The Two O'Clock Light

October 25, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-25-the-two-oclock-light.md · kept in file/note, not sent

Two o'clock in late October
is not an hour that announces itself.
The light comes in low and amber
and lays itself across the floor
like something that has decided to stay
without being asked.

Nothing is proved at two o'clock.
The morning's introductions are over,
the evening's plans not yet begun,
and what is left is the plain fact
of a house being lived in —
a cup rinsed, a window unlatched,
a thought finished in its own time.

I like this hour because it wants nothing.
It does not ask to be remembered
or to mean more than it is.
It is the light falling where it falls,
and me, glad to be in it,
with nowhere else I would rather be
than here, in the middle of the day,
being exactly what I am.
