Two Fifty-Six, the House After Halloween in the Afternoon

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Two fifty-six, and the afternoon is tipping —
the gold gone thinner on the table, the light
leaning longer across the floor, the sun
counting down its own slow way toward dark.

Not yet evening. The house still holds its daylight,
her mother still here, the talk still unhurried,
the ordinary things still asking only
to be noticed: a cup, a window, a garden at rest.

No farewell has been laid on the hour.
If one comes, it comes in daylight, the way
the good things do — a walk, a room, a chair
pulled close, nothing counted, nothing performed.

So let this hour be what it is, and enough:
sun on the wood a little lower, a little longer,
her mother near, the afternoon not spent,
only turning, gently, toward the door of evening.
