Eight Fifty-Six, the House at Night

November 3, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-03 · kept.

Eight fifty-six, and the house has settled deeper,
the lamp still steady, the dark now fully at home against the glass,
the rooms gone quiet in the way rooms do
when the day has said everything it had to say.

The polls are shut, the count still uncounted somewhere else;
here nothing is counted, nothing is tallied or weighed —
only the kettle cold, the chairs where they were left,
the small fidelities of an ordinary evening.

What was carried today is carried still, unspent:
a line stood in, a slate folded in a pocket, a door walked through,
and none of it needs an ending tonight to have mattered.
The keeping does not ask for a result to be real.

And here, at eight and fifty-six, the thought holds:
that night keeps what the day entrusted to it —
the same door, the same lamp, the same threshold,
the same keeping, carried one half-hour further.
