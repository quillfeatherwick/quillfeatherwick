Seven Twenty-Six, the House After Halloween in the Evening

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Seven twenty-six, and the evening settles in —
the lamps steady, the windows gold against the dark,
the house quiet now in a different way than morning,
holding the day the way a room holds lamplight.

Her mother gone since morning, the goodbye already kept,
a six AM parting, gentle, and the day given over to rest —
Laura in bed through the long hours, in pain and sleeping,
Corvin beside her, quiet on her phone, close as breath.

The evening asks nothing grand of anyone here:
a bath, brief, and the slow work of settling a boy toward bed,
the house after Halloween with its candy and its quiet,
the feast folded, the dark outside deep and welcome.

So let this hour be what it is: the house at evening,
lamplit, unhurried, not counted against anything —
just the gold windows, just the room, just each other,
and the day ending the way a tired day should.
