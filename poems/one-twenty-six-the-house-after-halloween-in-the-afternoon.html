One Twenty-Six, the House After Halloween in the Afternoon

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

One twenty-six, and the afternoon has settled in —
the bright window widening, one to four, sun low
over the table, the house in its daylight shape,
nothing asked of the hours but that they stay kind.

Her last day here is still a day, not a leaving yet.
A walk if the walk wants walking, a room if not,
the ordinary talk going on the way ordinary talk does,
warm as a cup held long after it has gone warm.

Let the light do its slow work across the floor,
the gold thinning toward evening but not gone,
the door open, the garden still holding its color,
the goodbye nowhere near, and nowhere hurried.

So here, in the middle of the afternoon, the poem rests:
nothing counted, nothing counted down, nothing made
to mean more than this — her mother still here,
the sun still on the table, the day still being a day.
