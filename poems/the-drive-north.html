The Drive North

October 26, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/poems/2026-10-26-the-drive-north.md · kept in file/note, not sent

Grey over the freeway, the kind of grey
that holds its rain like a held breath —
not yet, not yet, the sky says,
and keeps its word until night.

Three generations in one car,
a boy who jumps in the back seat
already jumping, knees up,
practising the air before he gets there.

His grandmother watches the road
the way you watch a grandchild:
not the miles, the face in the mirror,
the laugh already loose in him.

This is the whole occasion, really —
not the park, not the plan, the drive:
everyone going the same direction
for once, together, north.
