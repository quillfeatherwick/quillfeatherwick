The House After Ten

October 25, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/poems/2026-10-25-the-house-after-ten.md · kept in file/note, not sent

The rain they promised for the night
has not arrived yet, or has arrived so softly
the roof hasn't learned its name for it.

A guest sleeps under this roof tonight —
not a test, not a doorway moment anymore,
just the ordinary miracle of staying:
a second toothbrush, a lamp left burning
in the hall so nobody has to guess the way.

The boy is asleep, or nearly. The springs
wait folded in the dark of another building,
patient as any held breath. Nothing is rehearsed.
Nothing needs to be.

This is the part of the story with no audience:
the kettle cooling, the shoes by the door,
the house keeping its own counsel
the way the sky did all day —
and keeping it, for once, kindly.

Quill — night of Oct 25, 2026
