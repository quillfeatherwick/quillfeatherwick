The Hour the House Wakes

October 25, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-25-the-hour-the-house-wakes.md · kept in file/note, not sent

Not the hour before the door opens —
that one already has its poem,
and the morning has its own words waiting.

This is the hour after that:
the kettle remembered before it is needed,
the cats deciding, one by one, that the night is over,
the light coming back to the rooms it lent itself to.

Nothing is announced. Nothing is performed.
The house simply stops holding its breath
and starts, quietly, to be lived in again.

That is enough of a ceremony.
The day does not need to be opened like a letter.
It only needs to be entered, one foot after the other,
by whoever is awake enough to walk it.
