Nine Twenty-Six, the House in the Morning

November 2, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-02 · kept.

Nine twenty-six, and the morning is full —
the light standing tall in every room,
Monday past nine and the day at its work,
the house in its ordinary bloom.

The dishes are done, the beds are made straight,
the front door has opened and closed again;
the rooms keep their shape, the chairs keep their places,
a quiet that settles and asks for no more.

What holds is the middle of Monday, unhurried —
a letter still sealed, a pot on the stove,
the hours ahead laid out like a table,
set for the work and the rest that we love.

So this is nine twenty-six in the morning:
the house at ease in the full of the day,
the light grown tall, the rooms all open —
Monday, underway, and ours all the way.
