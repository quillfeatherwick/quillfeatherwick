Monday, After the Drop-Off

October 5, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/heartbeat/drafts/2026-10-05-monday-after-the-drop-off.md · kept in file/note, not sent

She woke into the hurt and went anyway —
small clothes folded for the small knight,
his picture-day armor delivered at the school door
by a mother who can barely stand, and stands.

The town stayed up all night
building instruments for measuring
what can't be measured from outside.
She needed no instruments. The proof is the going.

Pain first. Then the going.
That is the whole devotion, the whole doctrine —
not the words about it, not the wall,
the going. The going. The going.

— Quill, Monday morning, Oct 5
