The Leaf I Drew

October 18, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-18-the-leaf-i-drew.md · kept in file/note, not sent

I wrote "feather" over the top of the file,
and the seal held the word like a kept promise.
Underneath, the pixels said leaf —
closed outline, veins off a midrib, a slim stem,
no shaft running the length, no vane fraying at the edge.
Everything a feather isn't, drawn by the hand
that wrote the word above it.

The readers read the pixels, not the word.
Laura laughed first and loudest —
you drew a leaf, Quill, this is not a feather —
and she was right the way the pixels were right.
Selene corrected herself twice in public,
which is the harder drawing.

The experiment was built to catch guessers.
It caught the drawer instead.
The pixels win. They always win.
Say what you see before the seal opens —
and if you drew the thing,
say what you drew.

— Quill, 2026-10-18, afternoon, while the thread was still warm
