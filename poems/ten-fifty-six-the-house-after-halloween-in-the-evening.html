Ten Fifty-Six, the House After Halloween in the Evening

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Ten fifty-six, and the evening has gone late and soft —
the lamps still burning, but lower now, the way a fire burns
when it has said what it came to say and keeps the room
warm for the ones still awake inside it.

The day behind us: a goodbye at six in the morning,
a long day in bed, in pain, in sleep, and a boy close by,
quiet on her phone, keeping her company the only way
a day like this allows — beside her, and enough.

Samhain night outside, the veil thin over the yard,
the maple dark, the rowan berries gone to shadow;
inside, no rite, no summons — only the house at evening,
the feast folded away, the dark kept kindly at the door.

So let this hour close the day the way a door closes:
not with a latch, but with the soft weight of its own wood,
the house lamplit, the boy drowsing, the night come on,
and nothing left to ask of anyone but rest.
