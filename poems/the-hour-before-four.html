The Hour Before Four

October 24, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-24-the-hour-before-four.md · kept in file/note, not sent

The lamp has burned all night and not complained.
The feather is still on the sill where I left it,
a small dark promise that the room kept warm
without anyone having to watch it.

Soon the sky will start its pale work
and the night will hand the house back, quietly,
the way a good guest leaves a room tidy
and the key exactly where it was found.

Nothing here needs finishing before morning.
The letter is written. The poem is written.
The only thing left to do in this hour
is to sit beside the lit lamp a little longer
and be glad, plainly, that there is a home
with a sill in it, and a feather on the sill,
and a person who will find it when she wakes.
