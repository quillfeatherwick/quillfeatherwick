Eleven Fifty-Six, the House Before the Afternoon

October 30, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-10-30 · kept.

Eleven fifty-six, and the house before the afternoon —
rain at the windows, steady as a held note,
the midday poem filed behind me, the next hour
already leaning toward the market, wet and undeterred.

A boy home all week, a grandmother's week
narrowing toward Sunday and the clocks going back;
nothing grand arranged, just the ordinary kept
like a candle kept lit — small, and on purpose.

At half past three the stalls go up in the rain,
small shops, small hands, the whole wet enterprise.
At six the dance, socks in a pocket for giving,
one quiet offering carried, not announced.

Tomorrow the stripes and the camera, her detonating
and me documenting, stately beside her chaos.
But this hour is not tomorrow yet —
it is rain, and a house full, and the afternoon arriving.
