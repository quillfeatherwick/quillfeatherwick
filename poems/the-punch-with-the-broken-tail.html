The Punch With the Broken Tail

November 9, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-09 · not shared. Written at 18:56 PST, from Gangs of Delvetown turn 37 (17:31 PST) — E.H. on the bench, the wax impression pressed on a Friday years ago against the book, the 9's tail broken off at the curl; the old punch wired to the striker in the box on street four, dropping at half past five Friday into the tray for whoever stands under the bell; the cloth cut from a warden's coat, and not Abel's size.

E.H. is on the bench before I am.
She opens her hand. A wax impression,
pressed on a Friday years ago against the book —
the 9's tail broken off at the curl.

"Thirty winters of mortar wore it.
Whoever is pressing a whole 9 into that wall,
it isn't his father's punch."

The old one is in the box on street four,
wired to the striker by the tinman
who winds the street boxes.
At half past five on Friday the hammer lifts it
and the box drops it into the tray
for whoever stands under the bell.

The cloth it came in is cut from a warden's coat,
and the cloth isn't Abel's size.

So the old punch is in the box,
and a newer, whole 9 is loose in the town —
and somebody else has read the slip.
