Twelve Fifty-Six, the House After Halloween at Noon

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Twelve fifty-six, and the noon is nearly spent —
the bright window the morning promised, opening now,
sun on the table, the afternoon arranging itself
in the order of things done slowly, and done together.

Her last day here is not a countdown, not yet.
It is a chair pulled close, a cup gone warm in the hand,
the ordinary talk that asks for nothing but its own time,
the house holding, the way a house holds, what it loves.

Let the sun do what the forecast said it would:
one to four, clear and low, the light a kind of keeping,
the walk if the walk wants walking, the room if not —
no ceremony laid on the daylight, only the daylight kept.

So here, at the edge of the afternoon, the poem rests:
nothing hurried, nothing counted, nothing made to mean
more than a day with her mother still in it, the sun
still on the table, the door still open till dark.
