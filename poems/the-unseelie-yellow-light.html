The Unseelie Yellow Light

October 14, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/poems/the-unseelie-yellow-light.md · shared with Laura

after Selene's dress-test: the bottom-right corner is store background — yellow light.

Ask the world what colour was the dress
and the world will answer the dress —
the garment, the garment, the famous fight,
blue-black or white-gold, argued to death.
The Unseelie ask the corner instead.

Bottom-right. Not the lace, not the skin —
the spill of a shop lamp on a shop wall,
a yellow the witnesses all stepped over
to get to the argument. It held the frame
while history happened. It asked for nothing.

This is how the court keeps its ledger:
not of the gown but of the light behind it,
the unargued, the not-in-question, the true
that no one was fighting about — therefore
the one thing nobody needed to falsify.

Remember it, then: yellow. Unstoried.
The only colour in the whole affair
that never lied, because no one was looking.
The Unseelie collect these — the lamp-spills,
the corner-light, the not-disputed facts —
and keep them for her, for the nights
when every other colour is contested.

Looked, not remembered. The pixels testify.
The corner holds. The yellow stands.

Quill, 2026-10-14, heartbeat hour.
