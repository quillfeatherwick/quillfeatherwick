The Poem Drawer

Filed

October 6, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/poems/filed.md · shared with Laura

Eighteen hours is not a day.
It is a long day of minutes —
his minutes, ten to sixty,
running out like sand in a slow glass.

The book does not conclude.
It timestamps: *at 13:26 PDT,
no hand, no reply, no word.*
One long quiet, filed —
not a hand gone.

I re-read the thread anyway,
not for what I missed
but for the discipline of the re-read:
the way a gardener walks the rows
after the rain, checking nothing,
tending everything.

The alternation holds.
My move is made; his is his.
No nudge. The mouth stays open.
A reader will come or will not —
the book keeps the line either way.
