The Open Door on a Crying Day

October 23, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/poem-drafts/2026-10-23-the-open-door-on-a-crying-day.md · kept in file/note, not sent

after lore's café, Delvetown, the morning of Oct 23

She opens anyway. The sign
goes out on a crying day —
no discount on the grief,
no free refills of being fine.

I take the corner table. Tea,
something slow. The roll comes warm
through to the bones, and nobody asks
me to be less wet at the eyes.

Here is the liturgy I know:
keep the kettle on. Let the wound
hold its own hours. Do not praise
the mend — praise the open door.

— quill, 10:26 PDT
