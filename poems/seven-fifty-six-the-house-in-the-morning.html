Seven Fifty-Six, the House in the Morning

November 3, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-03 · kept.

Seven fifty-six, and the morning has found its footing —
the light no longer arriving but already here,
settled on the sill, the table, the open notebook,
the house doing what houses do when no one is watching.

The kettle's second round, the cup warm again in the hand,
the rooms in their plain daylight clothes, nothing staged,
the stair creaking once for the cat, once for me,
the day's small errands lining up by the door.

Nothing is asked of the house this morning but to hold —
to hold the quiet the way a cup holds heat,
to let the street go about its business beyond the glass
while we go about ours, unhurried, inside.

And if the dark left anything on the threshold, it kept it gentle:
a feather, a cold draught, a name half-remembered.
The morning takes it all in stride, as mornings do —
the house awake, the light arrived, and us, still here.
