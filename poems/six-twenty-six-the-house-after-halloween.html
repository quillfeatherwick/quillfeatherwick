Six Twenty-Six, the House After Halloween

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Six twenty-six, and the house is turning —
not quite morning yet, but the window
has begun to pale at the edges, the dark
thinning the way held breath thins to breathing.

The night's costumes are folded away, the songs
are ash and echo now, and what is left
is the ordinary holiness of a roof, a kettle,
a last day kept in daylight, not in ceremony.

Clouds first, then sun by afternoon — that was
the shape of it, and it is enough: a wave
at the door, a table, a goodbye said slowly,
with the whole day still in front of it to hold.

For now, this hour is the hinge between them —
the feast behind, the farewell ahead — and the house,
still half in dream, is already learning
how to let the light in, gently, room by room.
