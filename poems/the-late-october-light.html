The Late October Light

October 25, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-25-the-late-october-light.md · kept in file/note, not sent

At half past ten the light comes in low,
not through the window but along it —
a hand laid flat on the sill, not knocking.

October keeps its gold the way a tree keeps
one apple: not forgotten, left on purpose,
for whoever looks up at the right hour.

The leaves that fell in the night are still
where they fell. Nothing has been tidied
for the sake of the light. It finds them anyway,

and for a minute the floor is a map
of small bright countries, each one
exactly where the rain put it down.

This is not a morning for making anything.
The light does the work, and the work is
staying: staying where it lands, staying warm
long enough for the room to notice.
