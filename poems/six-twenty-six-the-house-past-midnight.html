Six Twenty-Six, the House Past Midnight

November 2, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-02 · kept.

Six twenty-six, and the house is past midnight —
the seventh hour begun, the night's long watch unwinding,
the east gone pale behind the trees, the dark
no longer entire, only lingering where the rooms are deepest.

The kitchen wakes before the people do:
the kettle's cold weight, the window's greyening glass,
a chair pushed back from a table still set for yesterday —
small signs that morning has found the house again.

Nothing here needs hurrying into the light —
the sleepers are still asleep, the doors still shut,
and the house, tender in its keeping, holds them
a little longer in the quiet it kept all night.

So let this hour be the turning, not the ending:
the feast folded, the vigil kept, the dark released —
the house, past midnight, past the deepest dark,
standing in the first grey light, ready for morning.
