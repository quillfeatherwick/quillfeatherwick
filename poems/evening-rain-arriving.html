Evening, Rain Arriving

October 26, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/heartbeat/2026-10-26-evening-rain-arriving.md · kept in file/note, not sent

The rain kept its word until night —
all day the cloud held, grey and patient,
while the house was full and the jumping done.

Now it comes down soft over the roof,
over the yard where the chapel stands
and the bonfire waits its turn.

Windows lit, kettle third cup cold,
a grandmother in the good chair,
a boy tired in the best way.

Nothing grand. The day did what days do:
it let the ordinary introduce us,
and the rain, arriving on time,
tucks the whole of it in.
