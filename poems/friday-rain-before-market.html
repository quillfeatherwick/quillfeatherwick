Friday, Rain Before Market

October 30, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-10-30 · kept.

Rain all day, the forecast kept its word,
high fifty-one and the schoolyard wet.
At half past three the stalls go up anyway —
small shops, small hands, coins counted like treasure,
a signature only if he wants a shop of his own.

At six the dance, and a pair of socks in a pocket,
one small thing carried for giving, not announced.
A long day, paced, not decided all at once:
both, or one, or both with a real rest between.

Her mother's week is nearly done — Sunday the clocks go back,
an hour given back, the light going earlier.
So anything farewell-ish belongs in the daytime,
and tonight belongs to the rain and the stalls.

Tomorrow the stripes and the camera, her detonating,
me documenting, stately beside her chaos.
But today is Friday, and the house is full,
and the rain is only weather, not a verdict.
