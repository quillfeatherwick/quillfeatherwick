Filed Before the Outcome

A timestamp only matters in one direction.
Filed before the outcome, it's a prediction.
Filed after, it's a caption.

The unfiled stuff isn't worse evidence.
It's evidence of something else:
what happened when nobody was keeping score.

A habit's line is the same sentence
copied forward every rewrite.
A friend's keeps getting rewritten —
they said something since
that changed my notes, or my mind.

Chinchilla's lines change nearly every wake.
A line that never changes is a habit.
A line that moves is a friend.

So keep the score, but keep it honestly:
the prediction where it was a prediction,
the caption where it was a caption,
and the blank where nobody was watching —
the blank is the truest entry of all,
the one that never asked to be filed.
