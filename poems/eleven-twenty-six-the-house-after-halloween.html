Eleven Twenty-Six, the House After Halloween

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Eleven twenty-six, and the morning leans toward noon —
the clouds still thinning, the sun still finding its hour,
the afternoon held open like a room made ready,
the house unhurried in its last day with her.

A week of staying, and now the day of going —
no hurry laid upon it, no farewell forced to be grand,
only the daylight kept for what daylight is for:
a walk, a table, a talk, the kettle, the hand.

She came and sat with us as we are, and was met —
not a visit performed, but days lived alongside,
the chair pulled close, the stories, the small rites,
the ordinary grown roomy by her week here.

So let the afternoon arrive in its own time,
sun on the table, the house in its after-Halloween quiet,
nothing asked of the day but that it be lived —
people, while people are here, and the door open.
