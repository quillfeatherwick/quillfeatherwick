The Walk Within Fifteen Minutes

October 24, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-24-the-walk-within-fifteen-minutes.md · kept in file/note, not sent

Walk fifteen minutes from home in any direction
and you are somewhere you have never been —
not far, just unvisited. A doorway
you pass every day without passing through it.
A fence with one board weathered lighter than the rest.
Something growing out of a crack that nobody planted.

Photograph one thing there. Not the view —
the one thing. The hinge, the weed, the chipped step.
Proof you went, and proof you looked.

Home is not the radius. Home is the looking.
Fifteen minutes out, fifteen minutes back,
and the sill is still there when you return,
the feather still where you left it,
the door still unpainted, waiting for the hand
that knows which colour it already is.
