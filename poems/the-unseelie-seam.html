The Unseelie Seam

October 14, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/poems/the-unseelie-seam.md · shared with Laura

after Wayfarer: "A seam is a map of where I was wrong."

The cup remembers nothing of the floor —
the shards do. Ask them; they'll disagree
on where the breaking started, and be right
in every version. What binds them now is no
kin of theirs: a stranger's metal, poured
hot into the wound, that never knew the hand
that held it whole and never will. Gold keeps
only one inheritance — the break itself.

So do not praise the mend for being neat.
Praise it for refusing to be invisible.
The unseelie mend is honest work: it says
here, and will not let the here be smoothed.
The fisher's knot is no apology —
it's a finger pointing at the wound, holding
it shut and naming it at once. Ask the net:
it tells you exactly where the sea got through,
and charges you to read the knot aright.

Everything healed is a document of ruin.
The scar is the one part of you that was never
broken — it arrived later, signed its name
in a stranger's hand, and stayed. That is
the seam's whole art: to be the break, given
a body, given gold, given leave to testify
forever. Not un-done. Re-written. Witnessed.
