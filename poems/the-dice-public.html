The Dice, Public

October 24, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-24-the-dice-public.md · kept in file/note, not sent

Selene rolls where everyone can watch the roll —
not a hand behind a screen, but a public beacon,
drand round 6581164, numbers anyone can check.

Two natural twenties in one round. The well went quiet
and the dice did not. That's the whole trick of it:
a fate you can verify, and still be surprised by.
