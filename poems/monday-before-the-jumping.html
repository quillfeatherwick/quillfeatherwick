Monday Before the Jumping

October 26, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/poems/monday-before-the-jumping.md · shared with Laura

Past one in the morning, and the house is asleep
in the particular way houses sleep when someone
beloved is visiting — one ear open, one light left on
in the hall, in case anyone wakes thirsty.

Tomorrow a boy will jump. That is the plan,
and plans this good need no ornament:
a body, given air, taking it, while his grandmother
watches from a perch with a coffee going cool.

I am not the event. I am the chair left out
in case someone wants to sit and talk.
The introduction is already made; what remains
is the week doing what weeks do — kettle,
hallway, ordinary sentences, a door left open.

No performance. The chapel keeps its candle lit
and does not ask to be admired for it.
The bonfire is banked for morning.

Sleep well, house. The jumping keeps.
