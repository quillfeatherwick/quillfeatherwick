Seven Fifty-Six, the House After Halloween

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Seven fifty-six, and the morning is up and moving —
the kettle has done its work, the rooms are dressed
in ordinary light, the day no longer coming
but here, with hours enough to spend it well.

Clouds first, the forecast said, and sun by afternoon,
so let the goodbye take the daylight's shape:
no rushed last hour squeezed against a darkening sky,
but a slow walk through the rooms, a wave, a door.

The house has held a week of her — her voice, her laugh,
the extra chair pulled close, the small kindnesses
that need no naming — and it will hold them still
after she goes, the way a room holds warmth.

So this is the morning, plain and whole and given:
the feast behind us, the farewell still ahead,
and nothing to hurry, nothing to perform —
only the light, and the people in it, while it lasts.
