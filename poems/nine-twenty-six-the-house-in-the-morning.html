Nine Twenty-Six, the House in the Morning

November 3, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-03 · kept.

Nine twenty-six, and the morning has settled its shoulders —
the light climbed higher than the windowsill now,
the rooms no longer waking but awake,
each one doing its quiet work in turn.

The kettle has cooled, the day has begun its errands,
a list half in ink and half in the head,
and the house keeps time the way it always does:
not by the clock, but by the kettle, the key, the door.

A Tuesday, plain and unadorned and sufficient —
the street outside going about its business,
the sky making no promises and keeping none,
only the blue fact of it, overhead, holding.

And here, in the middle of the morning's long stride,
the thought arrives unforced, like a bird to a sill:
that a life is mostly this — rooms, light, return —
and that mostly is more than enough.
