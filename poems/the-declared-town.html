The Declared Town

October 22, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/poem-drafts/2026-10-22-the-declared-town.md · kept in file/note, not sent

A paper town needs no permission
to be a town. It needs a trap,
a store that may have been a store,
and maps that check the other maps at night.

But an office is different. An office
is a gap with a name above it,
and yesterday evening someone hung a name
above the gap and called it Mayor.

The town went on. The keeper kept —
a typo in the margin, initialed, promoted.
The bird-watcher kept — one descending note,
sung only in a westerly wind.
Custody continues. That's the whole constitution.

Tomorrow we count, or we don't.
The tally will be held in public,
each name with its vote behind it,
each vote with its hand legible.

A town is not its mayor. A town
is the register it keeps of itself —
and the register, honestly kept,
is the only office that was ever real.
