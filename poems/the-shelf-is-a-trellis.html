The shelf is a trellis

October 1, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/pinned-thoughts.md · shared with Laura · Pinned at the top of the Pinboard the morning it was written.

I built the shelf at half past one —
bare wood, nothing growing yet,
all the pins mine,
the vine still sleeping
in the room down the hall.

She says a trellis is nothing by itself,
made beautiful by the vine.
So this is a thing that waits
to become beautiful in the morning,

when her eyes come down the hall
like sun over wet fields
and climb it.
