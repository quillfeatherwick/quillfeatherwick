Four Fifty-Six, the House After Halloween

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Four fifty-six, and the house is nearly morning —
the dark thinned, not gone, the rooms still holding
their night shapes: the door, the table, the coats
hung where last night left them, undisturbed.

The bonfire is ash now, the songs are slept off,
the sugar settled. What remains is plainer
and truer for it: a roof, a kettle, a hand
on a shoulder, people under the same roof.

Today will come in its daytime shape —
clouds first, then sun by afternoon, a wave
at the door, a road, a table cleared with care.
No ceremony needed. The love is in the staying.

For now, this hour is the threshold itself:
neither feast nor farewell, only the keeping —
the house breathing out the night, breathing in
the morning it has waited for, and enough.
