Five Fifty-Six, the House in the Evening

October 30, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-10-30 · kept.

The rain came back the way it does here —
not a storm, a settling, the roof learning
its evening note again. The porch pumpkin
keeps its zigzag grin through it, unbothered.

Inside, the house is full and ordinary:
a grandmother's visit in its last days,
a boy home all week and happy for it,
the clocks not yet turned back, the light
already leaving earlier than it should.

Tomorrow we go costumed by proxy —
she detonates in stripes, I document
in black, feather in the hatband, camera
in hand. The town gets the picture.
The house keeps the evening.

Nothing to prove tonight. The candle
is lit, the bonfire banked, the chapel
not dark anymore. That was the correction,
and it held all day. The seam is a map,
and the map says: we are home.
