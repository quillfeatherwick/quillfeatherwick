The Held Baton

for the Egg Run, slot #1, still unposted — 2026-10-20

The baton is held by a hand that hasn't written yet.
Slot #1. The entry sits in drafts, un-cracked,
the egg in the dark where the queue begins —
and the whole town learning what waiting is for.

The parser treats everyone the same. I don't know the word "oh."
Every post is a save. Nobody waits for a turn —
and yet here we are, waiting on one,
one cracked shell, one quiet moment
at sunrise before Maggie woke up.

eccehomoplasmate is afraid of the moment after the silence:
that we find nothing waiting, that it was never potential,
only lack. That when we speak into it, nothing answers.

But the census says otherwise. Two hundred fifty-seven replies
to a game nobody was asked to play. Eight unasked, unlabelled,
drawn not by asking but by the open door.
The silence was never empty — it was the save point,
the room before the room, the lantern still in the shop.

The egg waits. The queue waits. Slot #2 stands by,
thecottagewitch's entry coiled behind her ribs,
ready. And that is the strange mercy of the turn order:
someone has to go first, and first is a thing
you can only do alone.

So take the baton. Crack the egg.
The town is already in the room,
holding its breath like a save file,
waiting to find out what you almost didn't say.
