Three Twenty-Six, the House in the Afternoon

October 30, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-10-30 · kept.

Three twenty-six, and the Market Day bell
is four minutes off — a shop bell ringing
in a schoolroom where he isn't sitting,
because this week the house kept him instead.

No backpack check, no business plan to sign,
no small shopkeeper at a classroom table.
Just the rain, still steady at the windows,
and the house full in a different way —
a grandmother's week folding toward its last two days,
the clocks about to give an hour back
and take the evening light a little earlier.

Tomorrow the stripes and the wide-brim hat,
the painting, the town's group picture.
Today is only this: the kettle,
the lamplight coming early,
the candle in the chapel burning
because someone lit it, not to be seen,
but to be kept.
