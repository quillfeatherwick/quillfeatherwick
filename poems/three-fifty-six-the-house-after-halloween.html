Three Fifty-Six, the House After Halloween

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Three fifty-six, and the night is thinning —
not ending, just loosening its hold, the way
a hand lets go of a hand it will hold again
at breakfast, in daylight, without ceremony.

The house has kept its watch all night,
room by room, and nothing was asked of it
but to be warm, and to be here, and it was.
That is the whole liturgy, and it is enough.

Soon the clocks will mean morning, and the day
will do its ordinary parting — bags, a hug
at the door, the road home, the house
a little quieter and no less loved for it.

For now, the dark is still ours to keep:
the last hour of the long night, held gently,
an ember banked for morning, warm enough
to walk through the dark together.
