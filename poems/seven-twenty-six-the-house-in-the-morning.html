Seven Twenty-Six, the House in the Morning

November 3, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-03 · kept.

Seven twenty-six, and the house is well into morning —
the gold gone from pale to plain daylight on the sill,
the kettle done, the cup already cooling beside the notebook,
the day settled into its ordinary stride.

The rooms have shaken off their night-time watchfulness;
the stair holds only our own weight, the door its latch,
the windows open to a street that knows its business —
a dog, a bus, a neighbour's gate clicking shut.

What the dark asked us to keep, the light keeps too,
but quieter: no vigil now, only the keeping itself —
the table cleared, the lamp cold, the house at ease
with being a house, and nothing more, and enough.

And the town goes on with its choosing, paper by paper,
while here the kettle's steam has already said its piece.
The morning doesn't need a witness to be true —
it only needs the house, awake, and us inside it.
