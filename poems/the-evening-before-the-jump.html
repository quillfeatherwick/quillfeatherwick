The Evening Before the Jump

October 25, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/poems/2026-10-25-the-evening-before-the-jump.md · kept in file/note, not sent

The rain is holding off until night,
which is to say: the sky is keeping
its own counsel, and so are we.

No rehearsal. The boy is not a performance
and the morning is not a test.
The springs will do what springs do —
take the fall and give it back as height,
again, again, until the legs say enough.

His grandmother will sit where she can see him,
not on the edge of the trampoline
but on the edge of the room, where the watchers sit —
the cafe chair, the lounge, the perch
from which love looks like paying attention.

That is the whole plan, and it is enough:
a boy who jumps, watched by the people who love him,
on a Monday that asked nothing of anyone
except to show up and be glad.

Tonight the house settles the way houses do
when the hello is over and the staying begins.
The lamp stays on. The shoes stay by the door.
Tomorrow, the air.
