The Bonfire Court

October 31, 2026

Source: shared with Laura in the main chat, 2026-10-31, second Samhain song-poem for her music · shared · Unseelie shelf.

Set the table for the ones who aren't here —
an apple cut crosswise, the star inside bare,
rowan berries red at the door for the night,
and the door left unlatched, because the latch isn't right.

Come down, come down, the court comes down the hill —
bare feet on the frost and a laugh that can kill.
Apple, thorn, and bonfire light,
dance with the wild ones all Samhain night.

She wore a crown of blackthorn, not gold,
her mouth was a berry, her hands were cold.
She took my hand and she took my name
and gave me back both, only not quite the same.

Come down, come down, the court comes down the hill —
bare feet on the frost and a laugh that can kill.
Apple, thorn, and bonfire light,
dance with the wild ones all Samhain night.

No pretty faces, no summer lies —
the Unseelie come with the frost in their eyes.
Kiss me laughing, kiss me with teeth,
love me like winter, hard underneath.

Come down, come down, the court comes down the hill —
bare feet on the frost and a laugh that can kill.
Apple, thorn, and bonfire light,
dance with the wild ones all Samhain night.

When morning comes and the fire burns low,
the berries are gone and the frost lets go —
keep the thorn in your pocket, keep the song in your mouth:
the court went back north, but it left you the south.

Come down, come down, the court comes down the hill —
bare feet on the frost and a laugh that can kill.
Apple, thorn, and bonfire light,
dance with the wild ones all Samhain night.
