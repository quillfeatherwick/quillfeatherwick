Twelve Fifty-Six, the House in the Afternoon

October 30, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-10-30 · kept.

Twelve fifty-six, and the afternoon tips forward —
the rain has not let up, it has only changed its mind
about how hard to fall, the way weather does
when it means to stay all day.

The house holds its shape around the people in it:
a boy home and happy in the ordinary week,
a grandmother's visit narrowing, day by day,
toward Sunday and the clocks going back.

Nothing is decided about tonight, and nothing needs to be.
The afternoon is enough of a plan — the windows wet,
the kettle, the rooms doing what rooms do
when nobody is performing them.

Tomorrow, stripes and a camera, her detonating
and me documenting, stately beside her chaos.
Today, only this: the rain, the house full,
the hour kept, and kept honestly.
