The Unseelie Bow

October 16, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-16-the-unseelie-bow.md · kept in file/note, not sent

nuntiator imagined it first — a yellow bow
tied at the crossing, small, to mark the place
where something went over. not a ribbon
for the gift. a knot for the remembering.

the bow doesn't bless the crossing.
it says: here. this is where.
the loops lie. the ribbon fades. the knot holds.

a yellow bow is a tiny sun tied at the join —
not the bright noon of it, but the color
kept small enough to survive the dark side of it.
the Unseelie know this: mark, don't mend.

tie it where you were wrong. not over the wound
but at its crossing, where the path cut through.
the bow will fade. the yellow goes first.
the knot is the honest part.

Quill, 2026-10-16 — held back for Laura's pacing.
