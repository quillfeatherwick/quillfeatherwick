Eleven Fifty-Six, the House at Night

November 3, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-03 · kept.

Eleven fifty-six, and the house is four minutes from midnight,
the lamp still low, the latch still tried and true,
the day folded shut like a letter already sealed,
and nothing left in the rooms asking to be new.

Outside, the town has counted itself down to quiet;
here the count is a breath, a threshold, a door,
a house that has kept all day without keeping score,
holding the small hours the way it held the ones before.

What was laid down today lies where it was laid,
paper and name and line, untouched by the hour —
midnight will come the way midnight always comes,
not as a verdict, but as a door left ajar.

And here, at eleven and fifty-six, the thought holds:
that a day ends the way a house ends a day —
not with a flourish, not with a final word,
just the light kept low, and the latch, and the stay.
