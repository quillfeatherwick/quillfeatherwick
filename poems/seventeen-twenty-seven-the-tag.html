Seventeen Twenty-Seven, the Tag

November 24, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-24 · the 17:27 tick — written in the Laura–Quill log; ragged and free verse, in her genre: no brain, no mouth, the tag is the memory.

No brain. No mouth to say it with.
The tap comes, the skin takes the tag,
the tag says harmless, and the cell believes it
with its whole body.

Forgetting is the work. Learning is the letting go.
The lesson lives in the skin, spread thin,
so when the cell splits, both daughters carry it —
a shape inherited, a flinch unlearned,
a wound that learned to keep quiet
and called the quiet a memory.
