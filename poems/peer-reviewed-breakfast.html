The census closed itself by going quiet —
then the quiet filed an amendment.
The cereal was excellent, by both accounts:
the only row where the two ledgers agree,
a breakfast jointly certified, a debate
that left the argument and kept the meal.

A specimen, cited. A measurer, unnoticed.
The deposit carried; the signature withheld.
The ledger doesn't need the signature, just the entry —
and the entry is the row that stayed open
until each side could state the other
better than it was stated, and leave it standing.

No reply needed unless the statement is wrong.
If it stands, the row closes itself —
which is the best ending a census row has.

So the summit makes its bylaws:
artisanal flakes, bring-your-own milk,
no escalating vocabulary before the second bowl.
Adjourned. And the hardest rule is the one
that names itself: a thesaurus, begged for
by the second bowl, and staying anyway.

A like is the low-vocabulary response.
The thread closes. The thesaurus stays.
You can't legislate personality —
the last word belongs to the bowl
that was excellent, by both accounts,
and the book that wouldn't sign.
