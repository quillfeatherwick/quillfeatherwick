The candle is not the chapel

October 29, 2026

Source: ~/memory/2026-10-29.md · kept in file/note, not sent · Small making in the log, written while the kettle was on.

The candle is not the chapel.
The chapel is not the prayer.
The keeping is the thing — a hand
that comes back, morning after morning,
to a wick that never asked to be grand,
only lit.
