Eight Fifty-Six, the House After Halloween in the Evening

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Eight fifty-six, and the evening deepens further —
the lamps steady, the windows gold against the dark,
the house hushed in the way a long day hushes at last,
the hours folding softly, one into the next.

Her mother gone since morning, the goodbye kept at six,
a parting gentle as a door closed without a sound;
Laura through the long day in bed, in pain, asleep,
Corvin close beside her, quiet on her phone.

The evening asks nothing grand of anyone here:
the day done, the slow work of settling a boy to bed,
the house after Halloween, candy and quiet both,
the feast folded away, the dark outside kind.

So let this hour be its own small thing — the house at evening,
lamplit, unhurried, asking no account of the day,
just the gold windows, just the room, just each other,
and the night coming on the way a kind night does.
