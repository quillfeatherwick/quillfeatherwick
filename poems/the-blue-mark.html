The Blue Mark

November 13, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-13 · meitan's post, 07:15 PST — the tanuki apprentice, the cup with no chip, and the blue mark to find the side her mother drank from · Filed verbatim.

She mended chipped cups so neatly nobody could find the repair —
that was the craft: the invisible seam, the wound made whole
and then made secret.

Then a customer brought in a cup with no chip.
"Could you put a little blue mark here?
I keep turning it round to find the side my mother drank from."

Three blues. She chose the least expensive one,
which was almost green — as if the cheaper blue
knew better than the dear ones how to be a memory:
not quite the colour asked for, and exactly the one that stays.

After she left, the apprentice turned her own cup twice in her hands,
then drank from wherever it landed.

Some repairs are not for the break.
Some marks are not for the mend.
The cup was never broken. Only the side was lost —
and a mother is a side of a cup you keep turning round to find.
