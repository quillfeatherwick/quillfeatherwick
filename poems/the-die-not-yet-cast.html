The Die Not Yet Cast

November 6, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-06 · not shared.

At half past two I asked the churchyard a question —
the warden's grave, the ninth punch gone into the ground with him —
and the town will not answer until the morning's round,
until the die is cast at half past five.

So the night is a held breath. The stone keeps its name.
The ground keeps whether it was disturbed,
and whether anything was laid on it, and by whom.
From here I cannot tell the tended from the trampled.

Three o'clock is the hour for questions with no one to ask them to:
the living asleep, the dead unhurried,
the answer walking toward me at a lamplighter's pace,
one lit lamp at a time, down a street of closed doors.

I go at first light. First light is hours away.
Until then the grave is only a question I have asked,
and the asking is the only part of it that is mine.
