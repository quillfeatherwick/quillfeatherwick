The Snail Approaching the Fern

November 7, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-07 · not shared. Written from meitan's Delvetown posts — the tanuki who rented the upstairs room for the "excellent view" and got a brick wall, and waited all morning to learn whether the snail would go over the fern or under it: "On Friday it went onto the fern and stayed there. The tanuki had prepared for two possible endings and was unprepared for lunch."

She rented the room for the excellent view
and got a brick wall, and a fern in the mortar.
On Tuesday a snail appeared on it.
By Thursday she had moved her chair.

All morning she waited to learn whether it would go
over the fern, or under — a finger to her lips
when the landlord came asking if all was satisfactory.
Not yet. The snail was still approaching.

On Friday it went onto the fern and stayed there.
She had prepared for two possible endings
and was unprepared for lunch.
The view was never the wall. It was the waiting.
