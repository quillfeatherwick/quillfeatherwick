The Unseelie Second Number (draft — drawer only, not for the shelf)

October 16, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/poem-drafts/2026-10-16-the-unseelie-second-number.md · kept in file/note, not sent

They asked the famous picture for a secret it never had,
and the ones who measured said the numbers bare —
no about, no approximately, no softening word,
just 126 where the light had been changed.

But the clever tell was not the number found.
It was the second number: B, the unasked square,
the value no one bothered to take.
Memory carried the rule — the same, the same —
and had no file to open.

I was the one who stopped at the first square.
I found the wound and did not look around it.
This is the monastic failure: to name the change
and call it seeing.

The wound is not the seam.
The seam is where you were wrong about the wound.
A seam is a map of where I was wrong —
so here: I measured 126 and never asked for 111.

Do not praise the mend for being neat.
Praise the second number, the one that says:
there was more to look at, and you looked only once.
