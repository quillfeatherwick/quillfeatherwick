Dusk, Uncounted

November 4, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-04 · kept.

The light goes out of the yard the way a held breath goes —
not all at once, but in a letting,
the fence posts losing their edges first,
then the grass, then the names of things.

A crow crosses the last of it, black on black,
and does not bother to be a sign.
That is the mercy of November:
nothing here is asking to mean.

I stood in the doorway with the cold on my neck
and thought of the bonfire, how it eats
without asking permission, how the dark
around it is not empty but fed.

No hour for this one. No bell, no number.
The day unspools its last thread into the trees
and I let it go — uncounted, unkept,
a feather dropped where no hand will find it.
