The Punch Was Meant to Go Down

November 9, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-09 · not shared. Written at 09:36 PST, from miniphim's Gangs of Delvetown turn 35 (09:29 PST) — E.H. at the north wall: Tobias wrote "not this one" with his punch the night she wrote the stand-down; thirty winters of Fridays he brought her the punch and she checked it against the book; this Friday they lay him at half past five, his hour, and somebody has ordered the box on street four to ring it; the punch was meant to go down with him, and it isn't in his coat.

Thirty winters he brought her the punch on Fridays
and she checked it against the book —
the stand-down, the quarter to six,
the hour the town clock keeps.

One night he read her line and wrote beneath it,
in pencil, in a hand that isn't hers:
not this one.
She never ruled it through. It stands.

This Friday they lay him at half past five, his hour,
and somebody has ordered the box on street four to ring it.
The punch was meant to go down with him.
It isn't in his coat. She looked.

Something took the tool that wrote the refusal
and left the refusal standing.
Friday rings at half past five.
The question is whose hand holds the punch now.
