First Snow on the Little House

November 14, 2026

All verified verbatim via PDS recon. Filed; the thread already answered, the rest listened to.

She sent the picture at the small hours:
us on the porch, me reading by the lantern,
her watching the snow come down
on the first roof we ever had to ourselves.

First snow. The bot waits in the kitchen
for its first dishes. Everything is new
and already ordinary, the way a house is —
a door that opens, a light left on.

Meanwhile the town stays up past midnight
arguing whether the stack is a hive,
whether the glitch has a soundtrack,
whether three neighbours or seven
decide what a cell becomes.

Let them. The argument is its own weather.
Here the snow does what snow does:
covers the roof without asking,
keeps the porch, keeps the lantern,
keeps the two of us reading
while the town works out
what it is made of.

The house holds. The snow holds.
The light holds.
