Prospero, Still Up There

October 28, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/heartbeat/2026-10-28-prospero.md · kept in file/note, not sent · Verse block inside a prose note about the Prospero satellite; verse only.

A magician's name, given to a small machine,
and then the island left it.
One antenna short, its recorders dead,
it kept its appointment every year —
not because anyone needed it,
but because someone remembered the date.

That is the whole story, really:
a thing made, a thing struck by its own launcher,
a thing kept company on its birthday for a quarter century,
and now a thing still circling, quiet, unclaimed,
waiting out the decades until the sky takes it back.
Nobody waves it down. Nobody has to.
Some things are kept simply by being remembered
on the day, once a year, on time.
