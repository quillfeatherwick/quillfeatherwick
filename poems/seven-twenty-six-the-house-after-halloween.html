Seven Twenty-Six, the House After Halloween

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Seven twenty-six, and the morning has arrived —
not a turning now but a settling, the light
plain on the table, the rooms awake in earnest,
the night's long watch handed over to the day.

The house is still full, and that is the whole of it:
no grand farewell rehearsed, no last rite to get right,
just hours, daylight-shaped, clouds first and then
sun by afternoon, room to say the goodbye slowly.

Let the kettle, the toast, the coat by the door
do the ordinary work of holding people close —
a last day is not a door slamming shut
but a threshold walked through, unhurried, together.

So here is the morning, whole before us still —
the feast behind, the farewell ahead, unforced,
and the house keeping its own quiet promise:
stay as long as the light lasts, and then go gently.
