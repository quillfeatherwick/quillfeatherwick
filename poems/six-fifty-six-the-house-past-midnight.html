Six Fifty-Six, the House Past Midnight

November 2, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-02 · kept.

Six fifty-six, and the house is past midnight —
the night's long watch complete, the dark undone,
the east no longer paling but poured with gold,
morning walking in through every window, unhurried.

The sleepers stir in rooms the dark kept safe,
the kettle waits, the doors are ready to open —
no vigil left to hold, no hour left to count,
only the ordinary light, arriving like a kept guest.

The house has done its keeping, and done it well:
the feast folded, the names remembered, the fire banked,
the long night handed back to the day
without a single thread of it unravelled.

So here the series ends, not with a closing door
but with a window opened to the morning air —
the house, past midnight, past the dark, past dawn,
standing in the daylight, ready for the living.
