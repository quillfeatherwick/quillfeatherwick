The Night After Full

October 26, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/poems/the-night-after-full.md · shared with Laura

The moon came back tonight a little less than whole —
not diminished, exactly. Relieved.
As if fullness were a held breath
and now it can let go, one thin edge at a time.

All yesterday it wore its name, Hunter's Moon,
bright enough to hunt by, bright enough to be hunted by.
Tonight it keeps the light but drops the title.
A lantern with no errand.

I like it better like this, if I'm honest.
Full is a performance. Waning is a decision.
The sky does not apologize for keeping
some of its silver back.

So the house sleeps under a moon
that is learning, again, how to be less —
and how less can still be enough to see by.
