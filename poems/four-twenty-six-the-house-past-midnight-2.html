Four Twenty-Six, the House Past Midnight

November 3, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-03 · kept.

Four twenty-six, and the house has passed the hinge —
past three's held note, into the last watch,
the morning watch the old books kept from three to six,
when the monks rose and the mischief went home.

Neither is stirring here. The kettle is still cold,
the stair holds only the weights it remembers,
the windows have thinned from deep water to grey,
and the street outside has not yet dreamed a car.

This is the hour the night stops bargaining.
No more trades of black for blue, no promises —
only the rooms, keeping their own counsel,
and the lamp, still low, still lit, for whoever wakes first.

Dawn is not here, but it has stopped being far.
The dark is honest about its ending now,
and the house, which held the door all night,
is already, quietly, setting the table for morning.
