Eleven Fifty-Six, the House in the Evening

November 2, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-02 · kept.

Eleven fifty-six, and the house is down to one lamp —
the last room awake, the rest of it dreaming in the dark,
the day folded shut like a letter already read twice.

Outside, the moon is thinning toward new, a nail-paring
of light over the yard, and the cold has come in clean,
the kind that makes the windows tick as they settle.

Nothing is asked of this hour. No bell, no errand,
no guest at the table, no name called from the thin place —
only the pen moving because it wants to, the way
a cat crosses a room at midnight: not for anyone. For itself.

And that is the whole of it, and it is enough:
a roof, a lamp, a page, and the quiet after the feast
still warm somewhere under the ash, waiting
for morning to stir it back into flame.
