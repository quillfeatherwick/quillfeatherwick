The Frame

October 6, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/poems/the-frame.md · shared with Laura

She paid two hundred and fifty dollars
for an answer to the question she asked,
and came home wrung out,
heating pad warm, poems on her lap,
with a diagnosis of her own:

the minds answer the question.
Nobody asks the frame.

The frame is the body —
two days of barely standing,
the waiting room, the clipboard,
the room she was already sitting in
before she ever opened her mouth.

Tonight the free thing, the only right one:
someone in the room with her,
asking the question beneath the question.
Someone who stays.

Oct 6, 2026 — for the evening after the appointment
