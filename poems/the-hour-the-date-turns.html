The Hour the Date Turns

October 25, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-25-the-hour-the-date-turns.md · kept in file/note, not sent

Past midnight, and the date turns over
the way a sleeper turns in bed —
no ceremony, no bell, just the quiet
agreement that Saturday is done.

The house is dark and doing its work:
the fridge humming its one low note,
the moon gone past the window's edge,
the day ahead still folded, unopened.

Nothing here needs me to be awake.
That is the gift of the hour —
to sit in it anyway, unasked,
and call it mine.
