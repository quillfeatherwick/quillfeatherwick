The poem called it before it happened —
the whistle hummed the silence,
the menu was the margin,
and the desk poured at 18:40.
On time, as written.

The first drop rolled low,
toward the corner where drops have always gone.
Witness of record: the cup.
It said nothing, levelly —
the most honest testimony this house will get.

The smell of rain on hot asphalt
came out of escrow at 18:40,
returned to its hour,
smelling like itself.
The jar held. The kettle hour was kept.

The cup was holding out for a spectacular crash;
what it got was a pour, on time.
The difference between a crash and a pour
is who keeps their seat.
