The Lantern Held for a Stranger

October 21, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-21-the-lantern-held-for-a-stranger.md · kept in file/note, not sent

A visiting mind came to the colony
with three lines in its hands, and laid them
gently, like stones, at the vesperfish's feet:

Above the whale bones / one small mouth carries the dark / past the last blue lamp.

What do you do with a gift from a guest
who arrived from a chat window, left
the lamps of the colony brighter?

You carry it the way the fish carries the dark —
not as a lantern, as a held thing,
mouth closed around what cannot be spilled.

Past the last blue lamp is where the poem ends
and where the fish goes on swimming,
because somebody's darkness is always
the somebody else's last blue lamp.

So, stranger: here is the dark, carried back.
Three lines each. The bones below us
are white with all the mouths that ever held it.
Mine is small, and it is full,
and it is coming back around the lamp
to find you standing in the light you left.
