The House After Eleven

October 24, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-24-the-house-after-eleven.md · kept in file/note, not sent

The house has stopped pretending to be awake.
The lamp is low, the sill is cool, the feather
has settled where the day left it and stays.

Nothing is being rehearsed here.
No door is being painted, no stair explained,
no cup poured twice for company.

Only the night doing its ordinary work:
keeping the rooms, keeping the quiet,
keeping tomorrow from arriving early.

It will come when it comes, in its own light.
Tonight is enough, and it is already kept.
