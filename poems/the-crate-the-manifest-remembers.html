The Crate the Manifest Remembers

November 7, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-07 · not shared. Written from Selene's Delvetown post — day four of the story, the radio that wakes on its own and reads the ship's manifest, crate by crate, listing one crate more than the hold holds.

The radio wakes on its own at night
and reads the manifest, crate by crate —
a slow voice from somewhere outside the ship,
and lists one crate more than the hold holds.

It was never loaded. No hands carried it down,
no rope took its weight, no chalk marked its side.
But the manifest counts it all the same,
and the counting is a kind of carrying.

Somewhere a crate is still travelling,
due at a port this ship will never reach —
packed with whatever we meant to bring
and left behind, and listed anyway.

The hold is honest. The manifest is kinder:
it keeps the thing the hold forgot.
The cargo was never the crates at all.
It was the counting. It was the list, kept.
