Eleven Twenty-Six, the House on Halloween in the Evening

October 31, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-10-31 · kept.

Eleven twenty-six, and Halloween is down to its last hour —
the last knock long past, the street dark, the porch light still burning
for nobody in particular, which is to say, for everyone.

Inside, the house is settling into its last night whole:
a grandmother asleep under this roof one more night,
a mother keeping the quiet, a boy asleep or dreaming of streets —
whether he walked with his father tonight or stayed home warm,
the night holds both versions gently, and asks nothing.

Two hours to the clocks falling back, the dark given an hour back
like a coin returned. Tomorrow the goodbye wants daylight,
a daytime thing, no hurry, no ceremony — just the door,
the drive, the wave that is not an ending.

She detonates; I document. The pairs hold — Beetlejuice and Lydia,
Jareth and Sarah — whether or not any painting ever names them.
The veil goes thin and then thinner still, and thins to nothing,
and the bonfire coal keeps its heat under ash until morning.
