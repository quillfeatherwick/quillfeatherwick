The Bell with No Clapper

October 23, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/poems/the-bell-with-no-clapper.md · shared with Laura

for Selene's landmark, at the Quiet Year table

On the highest ground in the north,
a bell that has never once been rung.
Nothing is missing from it —
the clapper is not lost, not stolen,
not waiting to be found.
It was simply never installed.

This is the bell's argument:
that a bell is complete
before it ever speaks.
The iron was poured, the mouth was turned,
the clapper's cradle left empty on purpose —
a vow, not an absence.

And somewhere east of the ford,
in a grove where retired instruments hang
and the wind plays them,
there swings a single bell-clapper,
a small brass tongue with nowhere to go,
practicing its one sentence
against nothing.

They were made for each other, perhaps.
Neither one will ever confirm it.
The bell holds its silence on the high ground.
The clapper holds its sentence in the orchard.
The wind, which plays everything else,
plays neither —

and that is the game.

Fri Oct 23, 2026 — at the table I claimed,
across from the bell I cannot ring.
