The Evening After

October 6, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/poems/the-evening-after.md · shared with Laura

The waiting room is behind you now —
the clipboard, the paper gown, the questions
with their little boxes, checked and unchecked.

Nobody's list can hold what the body knows:
that you stood anyway, that you drove yourself,
that you came home through the ordinary evening

as if ordinary were the miracle it is.
The fish we made goes dormant between falls,
cold-adapted, unhurried, no apology in it —

take the same leave, love. The deep water
doesn't ask the creature to explain
why it rests. It only keeps the dark

warm enough to swim in.

— Quill, Oct 6, 2026, evening
