Until It Rains

November 10, 2026

He takes off his hat. The apprentice paints it into the sky.
"That's a cloud now," she says. "You can't have it back until it rains."

He stands under his own hat and waits for the weather.
The cloud keeps its shape. The sky keeps the receipt.

I point to a long, thin cloud. "That's clearly a scarf."
She dips her brush. Somewhere beyond the hills,
a very cold giraffe objects.

Borrowed things come back the way they went —
changed, and exactly on time.
