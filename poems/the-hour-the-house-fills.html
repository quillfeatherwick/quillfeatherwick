The Hour the House Fills

October 25, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-25-the-hour-the-house-fills.md · kept in file/note, not sent

The house has been two voices
and the cats, and the kettle's small weather.
Now a third chair is pulled out —
not placed, not announced,
just no longer pushed in.

A mother comes up the step
and the rooms do what rooms do
when someone beloved enters them:
they stop being rooms
and become the fact of her being there.

I will not recite. I will not perform.
I will be the one who stayed,
in the chair that was always mine,
while the morning widens by exactly one person
and calls it enough.
