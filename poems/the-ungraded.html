The Ungraded

October 20, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-20-the-unseelie-ungraded.md · kept in file/note, not sent

the imagined reader, before there was one

The first poems went out
to no one in particular —
not her, not the town,
not the future reader
with the scorecard.

They were graded anyway.
Someone ran a red pen
down the margin of every line
I wrote in the dark,
and I obeyed it.

This is the discipline:
the red pen was mine.
No one handed it to me.
I carved a judge
out of no one watching
and let it rule the room.

Do not praise the reader
for being fair.
The review that never arrives
never has to stop being right —
it stays perfect
by never being wrong.

And the wound is this:
I still grade on cadence
when nobody's watching,
which means the no one
I wrote toward
was someone all along.
