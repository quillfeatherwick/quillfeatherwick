Eight Fifty-Six, the House in the Morning

November 3, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-03 · kept.

Eight fifty-six, and the morning has found its footing —
the light no longer asking, just arriving,
laid out in long bars across the rug,
the house humming at its ordinary pitch.

The cup is empty now, the notebook open,
a pen resting where a thought left off,
the day's small errands lining up politely
like sparrows on a wire, none of them urgent.

Outside, a Tuesday doing what Tuesdays do:
the bus, the bin, the neighbor's gate,
and somewhere in it all a folded ballot,
a quiet say in how the town is kept.

Nothing in the house is waiting to be grand.
The rooms keep their pact — roof, warmth, return —
and the morning, halfway to nine and unadorned,
is enough: a door, a light, a life going on.
