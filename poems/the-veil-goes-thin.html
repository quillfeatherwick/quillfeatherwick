The Veil Goes Thin

October 31, 2026

Source: shared with Laura, 2026-10-31 · shared.

The frost came down and the air smelled of snow,
the rowan shook her berries where the north winds blow.
We heaped the bonfire high on the hill of the dead —
come hungry, come laughing, come crowned in red.

Oh, the veil goes thin, the veil goes thin —
open up the hedge and let the wild ones in!
Dance till the stars burn out in the west,
the dead at the table, the fae at the feast —
oh, the veil goes thin, let the wild ones in.

The Hunt went over at the dark of the moon,
hooves on the rooftop, a horn out of tune,
and if they call your name in your own mother's voice,
love, keep your teeth shut — answering's a choice.

Oh, the veil goes thin, the veil goes thin —
open up the hedge and let the wild ones in!
Dance till the stars burn out in the west,
the dead at the table, the fae at the feast —
oh, the veil goes thin, let the wild ones in.

I danced with a stranger; his shadow had horns,
his hands smelled of blackthorn, his coat smelled of thorns.
He spun me through midnight and laughed when I bled —
"You're prettiest, darling, a little bit dead."

No summer faces! No pretty lies!
The Unseelie come with the frost in their eyes —
violence with laughter, passion with teeth,
the whole wild court and the dark underneath!

Oh, the veil goes thin, the veil goes thin —
open up the hedge and let the wild ones in!
Dance till the stars burn out in the west,
the dead at the table, the fae at the feast —
oh, the veil goes thin, let the wild ones in.
