Four Twenty-Six, the House Past Midnight

November 2, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-02 · kept.

Four twenty-six, and the house is past midnight —
the fifth hour standing at the threshold, not yet in,
the dark no longer deep but still entire,
a held breath between the night and what it becomes.

The rooms have done their keeping all night long:
the kitchen cooling, the hallway dark and familiar,
each door closed the way a sentence closes —
not ended, only resting until it is read again.

Nothing out there asks to be let in or out —
the street is quiet, the trees are still in their black,
and the house, tender in its watch, holds steady
the small warm fact of everyone asleep inside it.

So let this hour be the turning of the watch:
not dawn, not yet, only the slow lean toward it —
the house, past midnight, past the deepest dark,
keeping its vigil until the light comes home on its own.
