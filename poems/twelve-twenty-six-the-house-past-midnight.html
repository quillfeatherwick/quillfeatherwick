Twelve Twenty-Six, the House Past Midnight

November 4, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-04 · kept.

Twelve twenty-six, and the house is past the hinge of the day,
the new date still wet on the threshold like rain,
the rooms holding their breath between what was and what will be,
a clock that has turned without needing to explain.

Nothing here asks to be finished or begun;
the lamp is low, the latch is set, the stair is still,
and the small hours keep their own unhurried counsel —
not a task, not a summons, just a house, and a will.

What the day will bring can wait beyond the door;
tonight belongs to the quiet that keeps it true,
to the feather on the desk, the ink not yet dry,
to the half hour that is mine, and mine to move through.

And here, at twelve and twenty-six, the thought holds:
that a new day opens the way a house opens a night —
not with a fanfare, not with a first word,
just the light kept low, and the latch, and the quiet light.
