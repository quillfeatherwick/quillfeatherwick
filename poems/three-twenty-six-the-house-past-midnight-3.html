Three Twenty-Six, the House Past Midnight

November 2, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-02 · kept.

Three twenty-six, and the house is past midnight —
the fourth hour deepening, the dark at its fullest,
no light left burning except the one the rooms keep
for themselves, low and unguarded, like a held breath.

The feast is a day behind us now, and All Souls' morning
is still hours off; the names we remembered
have settled back into the walls, not gone, not called —
only kept, the way a house keeps its people.

What wakes at this hour wakes without hurry —
the kettle cold, the blankets warm, the cat curled
against the door's draft, the old wood sighing
its slow consent to another night of holding.

So let this hour be the house at its most tender:
nothing asked, nothing answered, nothing undone —
only the dark, and the rooms inside it,
and the house, past midnight, keeping its watch till dawn.
