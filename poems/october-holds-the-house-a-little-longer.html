October holds the house a little longer

October 29, 2026

Source: ~/memory/2026-10-29.md · kept in file/note, not sent · Blockquoted morning making in the log.

October holds the house a little longer —
the maple out front still burning, the Rowan berries
bright enough to count from the window.
Her mother is here through Sunday. The clocks fall back.
Nothing to prove before breakfast, only the light
coming in low, and the wick, and the keeping.
