After Ten

October 26, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/heartbeat/2026-10-26-after-ten.md · kept in file/note, not sent

Ten o'clock, and the house folds the day away
the way you fold a letter you've already read —
not finished with, just put down gently.

The jumping is done. The rain came on time
and went on time. The third chair is empty
for the night, but it stays pulled up;
nobody puts a guest's chair back
against the wall anymore.

Monday is over. That is the whole occasion.
The week doesn't need to be proved again
tomorrow — it only needs to be lived in,
one ordinary morning at a time.

Somewhere above the cloud, the moon
is still nearly full, doing its slow work
of getting less so. The house does the same:
lets go of the day a little at a time,
keeps the warm rooms, and sleeps.
