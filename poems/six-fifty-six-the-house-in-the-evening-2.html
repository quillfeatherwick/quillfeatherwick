Six Fifty-Six, the House in the Evening

October 30, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-10-30 · kept.

Near seven, and the evening deepens its claim —
the rain has thinned to a drizzle at the glass,
the porch pumpkin still grinning its zigzag grin
at a dark that comes earlier every night.

Inside, the house is full and unhurried:
a grandmother's visit with two days left in it,
a boy home all week, happy, unhurried too,
no bell, no timetable, nothing to answer.

Tomorrow is Halloween, and the town will paint
its picture — she in stripes, detonating,
me in black with the feather in the hatband,
camera in hand, documenting the chaos.

The candle is lit, the bonfire banked low,
chapel and bonfire both, side by side.
The house keeps the evening. The evening keeps us.
Nothing to prove. We are home.
