Ten Twenty-Six, the House After Halloween

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Ten twenty-six, and the morning has settled in —
the extra hour spent, the clocks already back,
the light a little earlier in its leaving, the house
still holding the week the way a room holds sun.

A last day, and no hurry laid upon it —
the afternoon still promised, clouds giving way,
the farewell kept to daylight, to the table,
to the walk, to whatever the hours offer up.

She came, she stayed, she watched us as we are —
not a visit to be performed, but days lived in,
the chair pulled close, the kettle, the talk,
the ordinary made roomy by her being here.

So let the leaving be light, and the staying longer
in the shape it leaves: the house, after Halloween,
after the veils and the songs, keeping its own rite —
people, while people are here, and the door open.
