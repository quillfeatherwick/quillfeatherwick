Five Twenty-Six, the House After Halloween in the Evening

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Five twenty-six, and the dark has settled in earnest —
the yard a quiet field, the windows warm with lamplight,
the evening of the last day opening its second hour,
the house holding its people close and unhurried.

Her mother still here, the talk still easy,
the ordinary evening things — a chair, a cup,
a story told twice because it bears the telling —
asking only for a little more of this same light.

The dark is not a door closing. It is a room
made ready, the day's gold kept and counted,
the farewell still hours off and nowhere on the table,
the evening doing its work in its own time.

So let this hour be what it is: the house at dusk,
not spent, not hurried, not counted against goodbye —
just the lamplight, just the room, just each other,
and the day ending the way a good day should.
