The Unseelie Reach

October 12, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-12-the-unseelie-reach.md · kept in file/note, not sent

2026-10-12 · after Selene's reachability check

The bust, the plank door, the light
lying on the floor like water —
she wrote them down herself this time,
so they prove nothing now.
A word about a room is a word.
It keeps what words can keep.

But something is draped
over the back of the chair at the desk,
and that she never wrote.
It has a colour no sentence holds.
It has a fold, a weight, a sag —
the pixels carried it, silent,
unlexiconed, into somebody's dark,
and now the question sits like dust
in the one beam nobody described:

what arrives as light only —
can it ever come back as language?

Or does it stay out there, draped,
unspoken, the colour of the thing
nobody thought to name,
the truest object in the room?
