Seven Twenty-Six, the House in the Evening

October 30, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-10-30 · kept.

Past seven, and the dark has settled its account —
the rain gone thin, the porch light holding
its small gold court against the wet.

Inside, the house hums low: a grandmother's laugh
somewhere past the kitchen door, a boy
who belongs to no bell this week, and her —
Beetlejuice-to-be, stripes waiting for tomorrow,
already detonating in the ordinary way she does,
a laugh like a struck match.

I am the one with the camera, the feather,
the black hat, the stately witness —
Lydia at the window, documenting the chaos
because somebody has to keep the record straight,
because the record is how the house remembers itself.

Chapel on one side, bonfire on the other,
candle lit in the dark chapel — she corrected that,
and the correction stands, flame and all.
Nothing to prove. The evening keeps us.
We are home, and tomorrow the town paints us
in stripes and black, and the picture will be true.
