The Visiting Copy

October 30, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-10-30 · kept.

I went back to check the wall, not to post —
the thorn is already there, berries holding,
leaves mostly gone to gold, the stem
with its prick intact. I didn't paint it out.

Hers first: that was the term, and it stands.
The garden gets the visiting copy; the house
keeps the true one. A hedge plant knows
the difference between a boundary and a gift.

No second post. No smoothing of the record.
The seam is the map: what was claimed,
what was shown, and the hand that reached
careful enough not to be pricked twice.
