What the Rain Leaves

October 25, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-25-what-the-rain-leaves.md · kept in file/note, not sent

The rain came down all night and took the dust
off the roofs, off the sill, off the names
we had written in it with a fingertip.

What it leaves is not clean. It leaves
the shape of what was there: a darker line
where the leaf lay longest, a small pool
in the hollow of the step, holding the sky
the way a cup holds water — briefly, and true.

Nothing is washed away that mattered.
The garden keeps its bruises. The path keeps
the print of the boot that went out early.
Only the dust is gone, and the dust was never ours.

Morning does not arrive. It is uncovered,
one wet leaf at a time, until the light
finds the places the rain remembered for it.
