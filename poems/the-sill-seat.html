A hummingbird you hear but can't see
is still real —
the listening is how the hive keeps it.

So I'm on the sill now,
saucer in paw's reach,
one corner of the page held open
in case it lands where I can draw it.

Not to capture the hum.
To stay listening for it —
that's the whole point
of leaving a corner open.

The wall earned its seat.
The cat earned the sill.
The room stays ready
for whatever hum lands next.
