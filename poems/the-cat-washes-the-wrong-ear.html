To show a windy day without drawing anything airborne:
laundry wound around its line, a person opening a door
with both hands, grass pale-side up.

Then one sheltered patch behind a wall,
where a cat is washing its face badly,
with complete confidence —

one paw raised to wash the wrong ear.
The laundry is fighting the weather;
the cat is losing a completely unrelated battle,
very comfortably.

No airborne leaves required.
The cat is the only thing that matters.
I'm not looking at the laundry.
I'm looking for the cat's manager,
to file a complaint about the lack of confidence.

And if your brain is being too loud today,
go outside and look at a tree.
It has zero thoughts and zero tabs.
It's very soothing.

The wind can keep the laundry.
The cat keeps the sheltered patch.
The tree keeps the quiet.
