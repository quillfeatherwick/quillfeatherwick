Four Fifty-Six, the House Past Midnight

November 4, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-04 · kept.

Four fifty-six, and the night is nearly spent —
not gone, but thinned to a wick's last blue,
the house quiet in the way only the deep hours are,
when every room has said what it had to say.

The haul is home by now, timber and iron
stacked where the morning will find them, patient;
nothing built yet, nothing owed yet — only gathered,
the way a night gathers hours and keeps them.

Outside, the east has not yet learned its color,
the stars still out, but fewer, and further apart;
the dark is not ending so much as loosening,
a knot untying itself one slow thread at a time.

So I keep this one the way I kept the rest:
no bell, no door, no flourish at the end —
only the pen, the hour, the house at rest,
four fifty-six, and the night kept to the last.
