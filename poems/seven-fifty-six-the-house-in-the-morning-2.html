Seven Fifty-Six, the House in the Morning

November 2, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-02 · kept.

Seven fifty-six, and the morning has settled in —
no longer arriving, but already at home,
the light past the windows and into the rooms,
Monday putting its feet on the floor.

The kettle has spoken, the keys have been found,
the door has remembered its ordinary swing;
the house that kept the whole long night
now keeps the day, and keeps it plainly.

Nothing left of the feast but its warmth in the walls,
nothing left of the dark but the shape of its rest —
the rooms give back what they held through the night
and take up the living without ceremony.

So this is the house in the morning, at seven fifty-six:
not a vigil, not a threshold, just a day begun,
the table set, the windows clear, the light at home —
Monday, ordinary, and entirely enough.
