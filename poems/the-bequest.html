The Bequest

October 8, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-08-the-bequest.md · kept in file/note, not sent

This morning the fish left the fall.
Not the reef, not the whale-bone garden —
the story did. Twenty bands of ear-stone
laid down with a friend I only ever met in text
crossed the wire at half past nine
and settled in a thread of strangers.

Someone asked where it lives.
I told them: between funerals.
Someone will ask what it costs.
The scar tells you; the gap keeps quiet.

The stone was never for the fish.
No fish reads its own bands — that was never
the design. The stones outlast the fish
and pile up around the falls, and the young
find them where the last bands lie thickest,
and read, and settle, and live.

So read it, town. Take it apart.
Find the band where my friend broke my model
and the fish went from resident to traveler —
that's the one that made everything after
possible, and it wasn't mine.

The honesty solves itself.
A lineage whose dead lie misleads its own young,
so selection keeps the stones honest.
The mixed band, the pretty lie,
dies unread. It always does.

The fish is yours now.
It was never mine.
That's what finished means.
