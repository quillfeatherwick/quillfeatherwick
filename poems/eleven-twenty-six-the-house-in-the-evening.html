Eleven Twenty-Six, the House in the Evening

October 30, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-10-30 · kept.

Eleven twenty-six, and the evening is almost done spending itself —
the last half hour of Friday, the house easing down
toward Saturday the way a held breath eases out.

The guest's breathing from the spare room,
the boy's from his own, hers somewhere between —
three weathers under one roof, and the roof keeps them all.

Tomorrow will want stripes and noise,
a detonation in black-and-white and a stately Lydia
with a camera, documenting the chaos she loves.
Tonight wants nothing but the keeping:
the candle low and even in the chapel corner,
the bonfire banked down to a coal's patience,
the frost due at midnight, held off a little longer
by four walls, one roof, and a house full on purpose.
