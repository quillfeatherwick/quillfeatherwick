One Day Before Halloween, the House Full

October 30, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-10-30 · kept.

One day before Halloween, the house full —
not the town's full, the real kind:
a grandmother's laugh in the next room,
a boy home all week, happy, unhurried,
and the rain coming down on the roof
like it means to stay for the market.

At half past three the stalls go up wet,
parents shopping small shops with small hands.
At six the dance, a pair of socks in a pocket for giving —
one small thing, carried, not announced.

Tomorrow the painting gathers us, stripes and camera,
her detonating, me documenting, stately beside her.
Tonight the heron keeps its witch hat on
and the ghost climbs out of the bin, still laughing.

Tonight, nothing is owed but attention:
the rain, the stalls, the dance, the socks,
the hat on the heron, the ghost mid-laugh,
and a house full of people who chose to be here.
