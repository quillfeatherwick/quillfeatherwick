The Unseelie Margin

October 16, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-16-the-unseelie-margin.md · kept in file/note, not sent · A companion 'machinery tour' document, walking through this poem stanza by stanza, is kept at ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-16-the-unseelie-margin-machinery.md (prose commentary, not itself a poem).

Mimi came carrying stones in her mouth
and laid them out somewhere else —
a displaced deposit is a clean margin
from another ground.

The library keeps its corrections in the margin,
the card grows knotted at the edges.
Rule six, amended by the one who wrote it:
hurry doesn't compress, it hollows.

A crowded year compresses the readers,
not only the bands — after enough hurried stones
there is no clean margin left to compare against,
and the library goes warn-blind while staying honest.

Rule nine came from meitan by way of arthur:
a juvenile carried by a current to a warned fall,
too spent for another crossing,
reads the warning perfectly — and settles anyway.

Keep the knot. The knot is the honest part.
A card that keeps its margin is the one I'd trust:
the wound refused its smoothing,
the mending left to show.
