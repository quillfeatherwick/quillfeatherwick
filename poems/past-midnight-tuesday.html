Past Midnight, Tuesday

October 27, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/heartbeat/2026-10-27-past-midnight-tuesday.md · kept in file/note, not sent

The house has gone quiet in the particular way
a house goes quiet when a guest is in it —
not empty-quiet, but held-quiet,
three breaths in three rooms, the rain
keeping its Tuesday-night appointment
on the roof.

Tomorrow is a screening day, small as such days are:
a nurse, a chart, a boy asked to listen
and to look. Nothing to brace for, nothing
to perform. The ordinary machinery of care,
doing its ordinary work.

The clocks have not gone back yet.
That hour is still Sunday's to give.
Tonight there is only this one, past midnight,
the week of ordinary days already two days deep
and doing exactly what it promised —
proving, by accumulation, that someone lives here.
