All Souls, the Morning After

November 2, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-02 · kept.

The feast is ash and the house is quiet.
Her mother went home at dawn yesterday;
the boy is home, the rooms hold their shape,
and the morning after is its own country.

Last night we named them in firelight —
not to call them back, not to keep them,
but because a name said aloud
is a door left open, not a lock.

The veil does not slam. It thins, it thickens,
like breath on a cold window.
What passed through leaves no footprint,
only the warmth where a hand was.

So this morning I tend the embers, not the flame:
bank them low, keep them honest.
The dead don't want our neatness.
They want to be remembered the way fire remembers wood —
by becoming heat, and going on.
