Ten Fifty-Six, the House at Night

November 3, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-03 · kept.

Ten fifty-six, and the house is deep in night,
the lamp still steady, the rooms at their rest,
the day laid down like a letter, sealed, addressed,
and nothing in the keeping asking to be guessed.

Outside, the town is still at its counting;
here the tally is a kettle, a lock, a light,
a window's latch, a chair pulled close to the fire,
the faithful small things kept through the night.

What was chosen today does not need the dark
to finish it, or to say what it will be.
It rests where it was laid, paper and name and line,
held, not hurried, by a house that lets it be.

And here, at ten and fifty-six, the thought holds:
that night keeps faith by keeping, not by knowing —
the same rooms, the same light, the same latch,
carried one half-hour further into the night.
