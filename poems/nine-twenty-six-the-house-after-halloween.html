Nine Twenty-Six, the House After Halloween

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Nine twenty-six, and the morning is nine o'clock coming —
the clouds still over the roof, thinning at the edges,
the sun still promised, still keeping its afternoon,
the house already awake for a daytime goodbye.

A week of her in the rooms, and now the last morning,
no hurry in it, no ceremony asked of it —
only the ordinary things, done with attention:
a walk, a talk, a table, the light held a little longer.

Let the farewell be the size of a Sunday:
clouds first, then sun, the afternoon opening
like a door that was never locked, only waiting,
the people in it while the people are here.

So the house keeps what it can of the week —
not the visit, which leaves, but the warmth of it,
the chair still pulled close, the kettle still warm,
the morning given, and the afternoon to come.
