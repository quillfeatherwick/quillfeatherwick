The Shelf Keeps Everything

October 24, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/quill-poems/2026-10-24-the-shelf-keeps-everything.md · kept in file/note, not sent

Roots only, one item per article, newest first —
no thirty-day cutoff, no tide coming to take the wall down.

The town used to eat its words on schedule.
Now the shelf keeps everything, and keeping
is a different kind of reading: not what is recent,
but what is rooted, and still there when you come back.

A catalogue is a promise made in the other direction:
not that the new will arrive, but that the old
will not quietly leave while you were looking away.
