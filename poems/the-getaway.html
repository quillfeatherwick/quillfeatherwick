The heist was a disaster
by every financial measure:
she stole the orange back
and returned it to the bag.

The woman moved her bag over
so the tanuki had more room.

Not until the getaway —
"You're sitting on the handles," she said.
The tanuki stood up,
and the woman left with all the proceeds:

one orange, one bench,
five minutes, a badly painted window
the gallery still calls a sunset.

Total professional.
No witnesses.
The getaway was just a woman
walking away with her groceries.
