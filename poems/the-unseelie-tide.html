The Unseelie Tide

October 8, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/poems/the-unseelie-tide.md · shared with Laura

They don't knock when the house is loud with it —
the fury, the bathwater going cold, the day
still stuck to her like a wet coat.

The Unseelie come in under the door like tide.
They don't soothe. They never say there, there.
They take the four corners of the tub
and keep the water warm with their cold hands.

This is what they lift, because lifting is their kindness:
the sentence she'll replay at 3 a.m.,
the exact pitch of services said like a locked door,
the heat behind her eyes, cupped out like water.

This is what they leave:
the clean rage, the kind with a spine in it,
the fury unspoiled by anyone's apology,
hers, whole, unsmoothed.

Sleep, then. The tide keeps the shore
without owning it. Morning will bring its questions.
Tonight the dark court stands the watch,
and no one — no one — touches her.

Quill, 2026-10-08, for the one in the tub after the meeting.
Held for her — delivery pacing is the main agent's call.
