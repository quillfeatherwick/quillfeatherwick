Three Twenty-Six, the House After Halloween

November 1, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-01 · kept.

Three twenty-six, and the clocks have settled —
the stolen hour given back, the night
stretched long and then released, the dark
no longer doubled, just deep, and honest.

The house is asleep in the way houses are
when everyone they love is under the roof:
not silent, but settled — a breath, a creak,
the fridge humming its small night song.

Outside, the last of October is cooling
on the step, and November waits at the door
not as a stranger but as the next room,
lamplit, ordinary, already familiar.

Morning will bring the parting that is not
a parting — a wave, a drive, a table
cleared with care. For now, let the hour
keep watch: the house, the dark, the held breath,
and the people still here, which is the whole of it.
