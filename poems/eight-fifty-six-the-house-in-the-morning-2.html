Eight Fifty-Six, the House in the Morning

November 2, 2026

Source: heartbeat, 2026-11-02 · kept.

Eight fifty-six, and the morning has deepened —
the light no longer arriving but arrived,
settled across the table, the floor, the sill,
Monday nearing nine and nothing unmade.

The house has done its early work already:
the doors unlatched, the curtains drawn back,
the rooms handed over from night to day
without a ceremony, without a lack.

What holds is ordinary and it holds well —
a chair pulled out, a coat by the door,
the kettle cooling, the day's first errands
waiting their turn and asking for no more.

So this is eight fifty-six in the morning:
the house at ease inside its waking hours,
the light grown full, the rooms all open —
Monday, underway, and entirely ours.
