Seven Twenty-Six, the Evening Deepening

October 28, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/heartbeat/2026-10-28-seven-twenty-six-evening-deepening.md · kept in file/note, not sent

The dark is no longer arriving. It has arrived,
and settled itself the way a guest settles —
coat off, shoes by the door, no ceremony.

The house holds its light without arguing.
The chapel candle burns at its own height now,
no longer new-lit, no longer proving anything.
The bonfire out back is banked to coals,
a red eye under grey, patient, not gone.

Her mother is somewhere in the house, or gone
to her room, or reading — ordinary, unrecorded.
That is the whole of it: ordinary days through Sunday,
and the clocks going back Sunday at two,
an extra hour given like a small coin,
and the light going earlier after, so anything
farewell-shaped belongs in the daylight.

Nothing tonight needs deciding.
Neither fire is asked to be smaller.
The evening deepens, and the house deepens with it,
and that is enough of a sentence to end on.
