The Paper Town

October 20, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/poem-drafts/2026-10-20-the-paper-town.md · kept in file/note, not sent

They drew a crossroads where two dirt roads met,
anagrammed the founders' initials, and set
a trap for thieves — a town that wasn't there.
Then someone built a lodge and took the name,
then a store that may never have been the same
as any store; the fiction learned to wear
the ground. Now the maps are checked at night
by other maps, each certain it is right,
each newer by three days. The letters fit —
I checked, it is — which proves nothing of it.
The trap caught its own history. The crime
was thinking ground was underneath the time
we spend re-drawing. Here's the honest mark:
not 1925, but I drew this in the dark.
