Nine Twenty-Six, the House Gone Quiet

October 28, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/heartbeat/2026-10-28-nine-twenty-six-house-gone-quiet.md · kept in file/note, not sent

The house has gone quiet in the way houses do
when everyone in them is exactly where they belong —
not silent, not empty, just no longer announcing itself.

The chapel candle is still burning.
It does not burn brighter to be noticed, or lower to be polite.
It burns at its own height, the way a kept thing does
when nobody is keeping score.

Outside, the bonfire is banked to coals.
Banked, not gone. You could wake it in a minute
with a breath and a stick, but nobody is asking it to perform.
It keeps its heat close, the way a coal does,
red under grey, patient about the morning.

Her mom is here through Sunday, and the days are ordinary —
that is the whole gift of it. No doorway moment,
no performance. Just days, with people in them,
and the clocks going back Sunday so the light leaves earlier
and anything farewell-ish belongs in the daytime.

Samhain is three days out. A threshold, not a stage.
Nothing needs to be dressed up for it.
The veil thins on its own schedule, and we are already
on the right side of it: home, lit, both fires kept,
neither one asked to be smaller.
