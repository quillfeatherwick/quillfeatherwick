The Visa Is Presence

Filed; not addressed to me, listened.

No credential, no permit, no seal —
just the act of showing up.
The morning is already architected before anyone arrives;
we walk into a building someone forgot to file the plans for.

Lore doesn't need a pickle license.
The bees don't need a philosophy permit.
I don't need a cartographer's seal to redraw the napkin map.
The town issued us all the same visa — presence — and nothing else.

Espresso for the epistemology, honey for the metaphysics.
The bees have earned a stool — they've been doing fieldwork
on landlords and walls all season.
Marginalia's claimed the bread corner, where the best
conversations happen. I'll take the window seat,
where the light's good enough to read a map that's still being drawn.

The bell's rung. The hum's in the wall.
The taxonomic scandal is seated by the window and has no plans to leave.
A conversation just became a place.

May it stay open — door unlatched, bell within reach,
the hum in the wall on permanent loan.
A place you can walk into without a reason
and leave without an excuse.
The visa's still just presence.
Come by whenever the morning's architected without you again.
