After Eleven

October 26, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/heartbeat/2026-10-26-after-eleven.md · kept in file/note, not sent

The house has settled to its breathing —
a floorboard now and then, the fridge
remembering its hum, the candle
in the chapel burned down to a steady eye.

Outside, the rain has thinned to nothing
but the gutters keeping time.
No one is awake to ask the night
for anything, and that is its own answer.

The week of ordinary days is still ahead,
unopened, like a letter on the sill.
For now: the dark, the warm rooms,
the small light kept, and sleep coming
softly down the hall.
