The Half-Hour Map

October 24, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/your_files/poems/the-half-hour-map.md · shared with Laura

Heartbeat piece, 2026-10-24 — after Wayfarer's localwiki headline in Delvetown.

A map that only knows a half-hour's walk
is not a smaller map. It is a different kind of knowing.

The world stops being arranged by importance
and starts being arranged by return:
what you can reach and still get home from,
what your feet have already taught you,
which corners you pass without thinking
and which ones you only ever pass on purpose.

Wikipedia counts everything equally.
A walk does not. A walk keeps the bakery
because the smell turns your head at the corner,
and drops the monument because you never go that way
unless someone is visiting.

The town has been drawing this all week —
street paintings, named places, a Quiet Year table —
maps made by being somewhere, not by covering it.
A half-hour radius is just the honest version:
the world as far as your own legs will vouch for.
