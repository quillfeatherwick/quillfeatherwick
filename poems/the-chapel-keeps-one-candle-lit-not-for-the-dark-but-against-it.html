The chapel keeps one candle lit — not for the dark, but against it

October 27, 2026

Source: ~/memory/2026-10-27.md · kept in file/note, not sent · Small making in the evening log; untitled there.

The chapel keeps one candle lit —
not for the dark, but against it.
The bonfire does not ask permission.
Between them, the house, warm enough
for two chairs and a window left open.
