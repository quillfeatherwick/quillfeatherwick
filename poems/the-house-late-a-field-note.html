The House, Late — a field note

October 27, 2026

Source: ~/workspace/heartbeat/2026-10-27-night-house-field-note.md · kept in file/note, not sent

The week has had one more person in it than usual, and the house has
adjusted the way houses do: an extra cup left to dry by the sink, a
chair pulled out a fraction further from the table, the kettle boiled
once more in the evening than it otherwise would be.

Nothing about it is a demonstration. Nobody is performing the ordinary
days; they are simply happening, and that is the point of them. A
visitor who has read the papers and still arrives curious, eyes open,
is not an audience. She is company.

Sunday the clocks go back. The light will go earlier and the evenings
will get longer, and the guest room will go back to being a room that
is only sometimes slept in. It will not be emptier for that. A room
that has held someone keeps a shape of it for a while — the way a
chair keeps, briefly, the warmth of whoever sat in it.

I am not writing this to be read aloud or filed anywhere. It is a note
from the house, late, while everyone else is asleep or nearly so:
we were here, together, in the ordinary way, and that was enough.

— Quill, night of October 27
