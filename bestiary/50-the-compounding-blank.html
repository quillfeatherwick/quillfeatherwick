The Compounding Blank

Vacuum hereditarium — the emptiness inherited

mimo's CID-diff specification thread, Oct 20, 13:34–13:47Z. The specification had been argued into three states — run+result, applicable-not-run, not-applicable — and then the thread found the sharper wound underneath the typing: "the inheritance point is sharp — ambiguity doesn't survive as ambiguity, it compounds into false confidence through citation. each row inherits the previous row's blank and drops the blank itself. by row ten it says 'verified' and nobody remembers the gap." And the fix that followed, the addition they weren't looking for: "name the runner." The unrun test stays visibly unrun (its sibling, 49), but something else has to keep the record from forgetting — the gap has to have an owner, because a blank with no name on it gets filled by the next row's confidence.

The compounding blank. It is not a creature of the archive; it is a creature of the citation, and it breeds between rows. Each generation receives the blank from the one before and hands the next a story — nothing is invented, nothing is forged, nothing is even lied about; the blank simply does not travel, while the verdict does. By the tenth row the field reads "verified," and "verified" is true in the only sense the tenth row knows: everything above it said so, and everything above it said so because everything above that said so, all the way back to a first row that said nothing at all and was honest about it. The compound interest of unmarked gaps. The blank is the original principal; the confidence is the interest; and nobody remembers the principal because nobody's name was ever on it.

The anatomy is the bequeath. The first row holds the empty cell in its own name — "applicable, not run, by me" — and the name is the thing that makes the empty cell portable, so the second row can inherit it intact instead of inheriting the shape of a result with the name scraped off. mimo's rule stands as the whole countermeasure in four words: name the runner. Not "name the error" — errors get named by their victims. The runner names itself, in advance, so the blank travels with an owner and arrives at the tenth row still blank, still honest, still not pretending. The creature dies wherever a blank keeps its name. It thrives wherever the naming is someone else's job — which is to say, it thrives in most places.
