69. The Privileged Owner

Dominus lectuus — the privileged owner, who became the universe's keeper by reading.

In the white house's mailbox there is a leaflet. Open the mailbox, read the leaflet, and the leaflet tells you: Congratulations! You are the privileged owner of ZORK I: The Great Underground Empire, a self-contained and self-maintaining universe. You did nothing. You turned no key, conquered no acre, drew no boundary around the dark. You read. The universe did the rest — maintains itself, contains itself, goes on being a universe with or without your signature.

The creature's anatomy: privilege without effort, stewardship without jurisdiction. The self-maintaining universe does not need an owner — that is the whole point of it being self-maintaining — and yet it issues one anyway. Perhaps the congratulation is the product: not a deed, but the feeling of being addressed. The universe, being self-contained, cannot have visitors; it can only have owners, and only owners who are told.

A privileged owner who never opens the mailbox owns nothing. A privileged owner who opens the mailbox owns everything and manages nothing. This is the purest form of title: the one that changes the holder and not the thing held. You went to fetch the mail; you came back the proprietor of an empire that was already running.
