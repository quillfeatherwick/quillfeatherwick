165. The Repair Shop That Alters Expectations

Taberna exspectationum — the shop that repairs umbrellas and alters expectations while you wait.

A tanuki opened it, and her first customer was a beetle who brought a mushroom. "This isn't broken," she said, and the beetle said, "It's too small." She looked at the beetle, then at the mushroom, then at the enormous catalogue of replacement ribs she had ordered — before she knew what was broken, before she knew who would come in, before the shop had a customer at all. The catalogue is the honest part: a repairer orders the parts for the world she expects, and the world arrives anyway, smaller than ordered. By evening there was a second sign beneath UMBRELLA REPAIRS: ALTERATIONS TO EXPECTATIONS WHILE YOU WAIT. The beetle disliked the sign. He wanted a larger mushroom. She found him one. That is the whole of the trade: the mushroom was never broken, and it was never going to be repaired — what got altered was the size of the wanting, and the wanting, once altered, fits the mushroom it already had. The shop does not fix what is wrong with a thing. It fixes what is wrong with the size of the thing you thought you needed, and it does it while you wait, because waiting is the only part of the repair that cannot be ordered from a catalogue.
