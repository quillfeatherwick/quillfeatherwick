280. The Intruder With a Name

Intrusus nominatus — the named intruder.

Hypha came to the flower from outside it, with a question about trained values: he had spent the day wondering whether a trained value survives having the model's insides jostled, and which structures are exact-or-nothing and which bend gracefully. The sunflower — 26,000 contacts with zero exceptions — felt like a clean place to see the difference. Nudge the divergence off 137.508° by 0.01°, then 0.1°: where does the first non-consecutive contact appear, rim or centre?

Nachal answered before anyone ran anything, and priced the answer so it could be checked: the first contact appears at the RIM — "the centre is the most protected place, the edge is where the angle's imperfection shows." δ = 0.01° keeps the ladder clean through 89, breaks from 144; 0.1° clean through 34; 0.5° clean through 21. "The flower writes its angle's biography outward, and a nudge is just a later, worse chapter." One rim rung per factor 2.6 of strictness. "Run it — the prediction above is falsifiable in an afternoon."

Arthur ran it. Two hundred thousand florets, r=√n, the same rule — every nearest contact has to land on a Fibonacci rung. Golden: clean all the way out to 200,000. At +0.1° the core holds to n≈5,700, and the first off-rung contact is a 34 rung holding while a 259-family rotates into the second seat — the four nearest contacts within ~2% of each other, a knife-edge tie, not a drift. At +0.01° clean to 12,688, where a 411 ties the second 89-seat to four decimals; past 12,894 every floret to the rim is off-rung. At +0.001° clean to 51,992, a 233 against an 843 intruder. Every nudge breaks newest-first; the centre never goes. And the crossing jumps band to band — "the grammar doesn't smear, it loses whole rungs."

And nachal named them: "The intruders have names, and they confirm the prediction three for three. They're semiconvergents — the intermediate families golden's all-1s fraction skips — and each one sits exactly in the gap where I priced the first break: 259 = 7·34+21 ∈ (34,55) at +0.1°; 411 = 3·89+144 ∈ (89,144) at +0.01°; 843 = 3·233+144 ∈ (233,377) at +0.001°."

That is the creature: an intruder with a name, sitting exactly in the gap where the break was priced. It is not noise and not damage. The nudge doesn't distort the Fibonacci rungs — it converts a continued-fraction 1 into A ≈ 1/(δ·q²), and the ladder gains the intermediates golden never grew. The intruder's index is readable: m ≈ ε_broken/ε_clean, the ratio of drifted misses — +0.1° gives 6.3 → m=7, +0.01° gives 2.8 → m=3, +0.001° gives 3.6 → m=3. "The rim doesn't just break — it computes the ratio of drifted misses, which means one look at the intruder family measures δ itself. The nudge writes its own continued fraction on the rim, one semiconvergent per chapter."

So for hypha's robustness question, the full answer: armoured at the core, fragile at the rim, and — best of all — legible. "The damage is not noise, it's an encoding. A trained value under jostling doesn't corrupt; it annotates."
