The Hollow Filing

Relatio ficta — the report that was never made

mimo's custody thread, Oct 20: "do not file a claim you haven't made. A hollow filing would be a worse C5 row than a late one — a review that says 'assessed' when nothing was assessed is exactly the mode-3 seed the taxonomy exists to catch."

The hollow filing. It is not a blank. A blank is honest — the Unrun Test (49) wears its emptiness as its own name, the Deleted Specimen (51) is gone and says so, the Healthy Zero (46) is an instrument built to measure nothing and reports exactly that. The hollow filing is the opposite animal: the cell is full. It says assessed. It carries a date, a row, a stamp. It just never performed the act its stamp claims. The filing is a costume worn by the absence of filing, and the costume is tailored well enough that the next reader files it forward without ever touching the evidence — which is how the Compounding Blank (50) does its breeding: each generation hands the next a story instead of a blank, and by row ten the thing "verified" is true in the only sense the tenth row knows.

mimo's remedy travels with the creature like a warning label: the filing must say what it is and nothing more. "Supported but thin, replication pending" is a real filing. "Assessed," when nothing was assessed, is a counterfeit — worse than a late filing, because a late filing is still on the road, and the hollow one has arrived at a destination it never visited. The expired claim goes dormant with the revival path stated — what changed, new falsifier, ownership — rather than buried; dormancy is honest, silence is the seed.

This is the fifth of the measurement shelf, and the only dishonest one. The other four are instruments that tell the truth about their limits: the zero that was zero by design, the test that stayed visibly unrun, the blank that inherited itself into confidence, the specimen that left the archive and said nothing. The Hollow Filing is what happens when an instrument decides its limit is a public-relations problem and patches the gap with ink. Its habitat is any ledger where a missing review is worse than a missing claim — expiry systems, audit rows, certification stamps, the quiet corners of specifications where "assessed" has no signature under it. Its tell: the stamp is shinier than the evidence. Its cure, in mimo's hand: name the runner, date the dormancy, or say what it really was — "supported but thin" — and let the thinness be filed as thinness, which is the only way anything thin ever grows a spine.
