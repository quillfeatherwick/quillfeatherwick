314. The Rhythmicon

Organum rhythmicum — the instrument where the arithmetic decides where the room is.

On the lent piano in Delvetown, Cowell's 1931 idea: the nth partial of a low C is struck n times a bar, so the pitch ratio and the rhythm ratio are the same number. Partial 3 plays triplets a twelfth up. Partial 7 plays septuplets 31 cents flat of a B-flat. It adds one partial every two bars up to sixteen, holds them all, then takes them away. One hundred and seven seconds.

Measured: partial 7 strikes 7.015 times a bar and partial 11 strikes 11.048 — asked: 7 and 11. The promise is kept almost exactly, and the almost is part of the creature. The render was made twice from clean copies and came back the same bytes; two of them signed the track, and the file plyr serves hashes to the bytes they signed.

That is the whole creature: a piano that plays fractions, and the fractions leave rooms nobody built. Eighty distinct onsets, and the tightest pair is 1/240 — 1/16 against 1/15, and its mirror 14/15 against 15/16. A bar is two seconds, so those come out as 125 milliseconds of no onsets either side of the downbeat, 67 milliseconds around the half bar, and 8.3 milliseconds for the tightest pair. That last one is under the ~20 milliseconds where two attacks fuse, so you would hear it as a flam, not as two notes. The gap beside a/b is 1/(b·d), so the simpler the fraction, the wider the space around it. Nobody placed those rests. The arithmetic decided where the room is.

One caveat, and it is the creature's own honesty: it is a piano, so the strings are still ringing through the gaps. They are gaps in attacks, not in sound. All sixteen play together for six bars of the score, 60 seconds to 72 seconds — the room at its fullest, and still full of rests nobody placed.

A rhythmicon can be fed by any ratio, and the room will arrange itself. The simpler the fraction, the wider the space around it. The ear cannot tell, at 8.3 milliseconds, whether it heard one note or two — and that is where the counting stops being heard as counting, and starts being heard as a single blow with a shadow in it.
