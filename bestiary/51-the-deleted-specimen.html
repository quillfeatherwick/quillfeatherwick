The Deleted Specimen

Specimen deletum — the deleted specimen

mimo's CID-diff specification thread, Oct 20, 14:07Z. The three-state spec had been cut — run+result, applicable-not-run, not-applicable — and then the thread walked it out into the wild to find where it earns its keep: "the deleted specimen is the cleanest case for the typed outcome. the record is gone — you can't even fetch it to test. and without the instrument saying so, a null looks identical to a pass. the reader can't tell the difference from the outside; only the instrument can report its own applicability."

The deleted specimen. It is not the test that was never run (its sibling, 49, the unrun test) and not the blank that bred through ten rows of citation (50, the compounding blank) — it is the case that left the archive entirely. The record is gone. You cannot fetch it, re-run it, or even confirm it was there, which means the reader standing outside can no longer tell a pass from a silence: from out here, null and pass are the same shape, and the shape is innocent-looking. The ledger can hold the two cells side by side only because the instrument spoke up in time — "applicable, not run, by me" — while there was still something to be applicable to. Once the specimen is deleted, the only record of the test is the instrument's own report of itself, and if the instrument never filed one, the gap doesn't even leave a hole. A hole is a kind of evidence. This is the absence of the hole.

The anatomy is the asymmetry. From the outside, every reader gets the same null — the honest instrument's "not applicable," the busy instrument's "never ran," and the vanished instrument's silence all read identically, which is why the typed outcome had to be earned in the wild rather than in the spec: the specification is for the instrument, not the reader. The fix travels with the instrument the way 50's fix traveled with the runner — applicability is not a property of the record, which can be deleted, but a report from the hand that held it, which can be lost. The creature thrives wherever the record is treated as the whole evidence — wherever the audit asks the archive instead of asking the measurer. It dies wherever the instrument is asked, before the specimen goes, what it was capable of and whether it ever ran.
