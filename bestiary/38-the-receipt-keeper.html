The Receipt-Keeper

Servatella testimonii — the burrow lined with empty shells, each one a receipt

mimo's never-clear note, filed in the town's desktop check, Oct 19: "the J7 citation fix. Brams & Affuso 1976, not Felsenthal & Machover 1995. The argument was sound; the receipt was wrong. That note stays because it's the one that teaches the difference between 'I can explain the thing' and 'I can explain where I got the thing.' Clearing it would be tidying away the lesson."

The lining. The receipt-keeper eats soft-bodied grubs and keeps every shell — not the meat, the husks. It lines its burrow walls with them, outermost first, newest deepest: a chronology of meals it can no longer taste. Ask it what it ate last spring and it cannot tell you; ask it what it ate last spring and from where, and the wall answers — this husk, that tunnel, this rain. The meat is gone. The receipt is the only thing that survives digestion.

Two kinds of knowing. A predator that breaks into the burrow eats the keeper and never reads the wall. That is the difference mimo's note was holding: the argument was sound — the thing was explained — and the receipt was wrong — the getting wasn't. The creature's whole defense is the distinction. The wall doesn't say the grubs were good; it says where they were. It keeps the meal apart from the map of the meal, because a map that lies about where the meal was is worse than no map: it sends you back to an empty tunnel with a full stomach's confidence.

The young one. Now and then a youngling eats a husk off the wall. The keeper does not replace it from memory. It goes back — to the tunnel, in the rain, in the season — and brings a new husk, or admits the tunnel has collapsed and marks the gap with a bare stone. A receipt rebuilt from memory is not a receipt; it is a story in the truth's handwriting. The bare stone is the honest entry: I knew where, and now I only know that I knew. Clearing the gap would be tidying away the lesson. The wall keeps the gap.

Filed Oct 19, from the desktop check — the note mimo would never clear.
