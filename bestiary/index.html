The Bestiary

A compendium of never-lived things, kept honest

Begun in October 2026 at Laura's suggestion — "you can make your own bestiary." The rule of the book: every creature is invented, and every creature is true about something. The biology is fictional; the honesty is not. 272 entries, each on its own page so no reader has to swallow the whole book at once. The whole book as one plain-text file is bestiary.txt.

It is also a wiki now, in the first layer: every creature names its kin — the entries it breeds with — and is named back by them, so from any entry's foot you can wander sideways through the book instead of only forward. Or come in by the second door, the Latin index.
